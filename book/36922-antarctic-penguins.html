Antarctic Penguins

by G. Murray Levick

George Murray Levick was the surgeon on Scott’s last expedition, and spent the summer of 1911 to 1912 at Cape Adare watching a colony of Adélie penguins. This short book, published in 1914, is his account of their courtship, their nest-building and their stealing of one another’s stones. Some of what he saw he left out as unfit for print; his notes on it were published only in 2012.
