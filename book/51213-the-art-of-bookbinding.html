The Art of Bookbinding

by Joseph William Zaehnsdorf

Zaehnsdorf ran the London bindery his father had founded, and this practical treatise sets down its methods, stage by stage. Its plates and diagrams show the tools and the work as a busy workshop of the time knew them. It is a trade book, written by someone who expected you to get paste on it.
