Opticks

by Isaac Newton

Newton’s book on light, first published in 1704: white light parted by a prism into colours that cannot be parted again. He wrote it in English, unlike the Principia, and ended it with a list of open questions. The camera obscura is older than Newton; what it shows, he explained.
