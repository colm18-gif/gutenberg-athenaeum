No Hiding Place

by Richard Rein Smith

A man on the run discovers that the further he flees, the fewer places remain willing to take him in. It is a slight, forgotten thriller by a writer who left very little other trace of himself in print. I shelve it here less for its craft than for the fact that someone, once, thought it worth finishing.
