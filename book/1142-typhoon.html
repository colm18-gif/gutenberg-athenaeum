Typhoon

by Joseph Conrad

Captain MacWhirr, a man with no imagination at all, steers his steamer straight into a typhoon in the China Seas with two hundred passengers below. Conrad, who spent twenty years at sea, wrote the storm from experience. The captain’s stubbornness turns out to be its own kind of courage.
