The Satyricon, Dinner of Trimalchio

by Petronius Arbiter

The most famous dinner in Latin literature, from Petronius’s novel of the reign of Nero: the freedman Trimalchio, vulgar and very rich, feeds his guests course after absurd course. Among the first things on the table are dormice sprinkled with honey and poppy seed. This is W. C. Firebaugh’s translation, and the novel around it is not for children.
