Narrative of the Surveying Voyages of His Majesty’s Ships Adventure and Beagle

by Robert Fitzroy

Robert FitzRoy commanded the Beagle on the voyage that carried Darwin, and this is the narrative he wrote of it. His officers surveyed the Galápagos in September and October 1835, and the Admiralty’s chart of the islands was made from it. The captain was not persuaded by what his passenger later made of the voyage.
