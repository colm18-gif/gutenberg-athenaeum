Lalla Rookh

by Thomas Moore

Thomas Moore’s publishers, Longmans, agreed to pay him three thousand pounds for Lalla Rookh before he had written it, the most ever paid for a poem, and it came out in 1817. Its four verse tales are told by a young poet to a Mughal princess, the daughter of Aurangzeb, on her journey from Delhi to Kashmir to marry a king she has never seen. The poet turns out to be the king.
