The Frozen Deep

by Wilkie Collins

Wilkie Collins wrote The Frozen Deep as a play with Charles Dickens, about an Arctic expedition lost in the ice, a few years after John Rae’s news of Franklin’s men; Dickens played the lead in 1857. Collins later made the play into this story. It is a melodrama of love, jealousy and the ice.
