The Ramayan of Valmiki

by Valmiki

Ralph T. H. Griffith’s verse translation of the Ramayana, published in 1870 to 1874, one of the first in English to cover the whole poem. It tells the story of Rama, his exile, the abduction of Sita and the war against Ravana. It is read and performed across Asia and has been for two thousand years.
