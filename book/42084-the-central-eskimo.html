The Central Eskimo

by Franz Boas

Franz Boas spent a year on Baffin Island in 1883 and 1884, travelling with the Inuit, and this is the long report he wrote of their way of life for the Bureau of Ethnology. It is the founding work of a whole way of studying a people from the inside. Its maps of the coast were drawn with Inuit help.
