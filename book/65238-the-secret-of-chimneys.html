The Secret of Chimneys

by Agatha Christie

A young woman is drawn into international intrigue involving a stolen manuscript and a small, unstable Balkan kingdom’s throne. Christie clearly enjoyed writing this lighter, more swashbuckling register than her stricter puzzle mysteries allow. Take it as an adventure with a mystery folded inside it, rather than the other way round.
