Micromegas

by Voltaire

Voltaire’s giant comes from a planet circling Sirius and is eight leagues tall; with a companion from Saturn he visits the Earth and finds, in the Baltic, a ship of philosophers so small that he has to look at them through a diamond. The tiny creatures turn out to know a great deal about the measure of things and nothing about themselves. Voltaire had lived in England and read Swift, and the tale of 1752 owes Gulliver a good deal.
