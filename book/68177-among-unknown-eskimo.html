Among Unknown Eskimo

by Julian W. Bilby

Julian Bilby lived among the Inuit of Baffin Island for twelve years, and this is his account of them, published in 1923. It is affectionate, detailed and sometimes of its time. Its title, like others on this shelf, uses the word then in use.
