The Sword Speech

by Thomas Francis Meagher

In July 1846 Thomas Francis Meagher, then twenty-two, rose in Conciliation Hall in Dublin against the demand that the Repeal Association renounce physical force for ever. He would not abhor the sword, he said, and the Young Irelanders walked out with him; the speech made his name. This is the speech as Wikisource gives it.
