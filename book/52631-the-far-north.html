The Far North

by Elisha Kent Kane

Elisha Kent Kane’s second voyage, of 1853 to 1855, took the brig Advance up the channel between Greenland and Ellesmere Island further north than ships had gone. She was frozen in for two winters, and in the end abandoned, and her men came home by boat and sledge, helped by the Inuit of Etah. This is his own account, in a shorter edition printed in Edinburgh in 1879.
