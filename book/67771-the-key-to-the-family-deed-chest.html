The Key to the Family Deed Chest

by Emma Elizabeth Thoyts

A guide to reading the old papers a family keeps, its deeds, wills and court rolls, written in hands and abbreviations that look like a cipher to anyone who has not learned them. Miss Thoyts teaches the letters one by one, with facsimiles. Most of what looks unreadable is only unfamiliar; the manuscript on the lectern is the rare exception.
