The Mysterious Locket

by Ruth Lynn

A stolen piece of jewelry sets off a chain of blackmail, disguise, and revelation in a story that was, by all evidence, barely read even on its first printing. I have found almost nothing about its author beyond this single surviving title. Some books in this catalogue exist mostly to prove that obscurity does not require merit to earn it.
