Impressions of America, Volume 2

by Tyrone Power

The second volume of Tyrone Power’s American travels, from the years 1833 to 1835, when he played the theatres of the eastern cities and wrote down what he saw between engagements. Power wrote plays as well as acting in them. In 1841 he sailed home from New York on the steamship President, which was lost at sea with everyone aboard.
