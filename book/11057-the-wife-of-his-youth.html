The Wife of His Youth

by Charles W. Chesnutt

Stories of the colour line, opening with a respectable man about to propose at his own ball when an old woman arrives looking for the husband she married in slavery. Chesnutt published them in 1899. The title story turns on what he chooses to say about her in front of his guests.
