The Stolen White Elephant

by Mark Twain

Mark Twain’s collection of 1882, with the title story of a detective hunt for a sacred elephant lost in New Jersey. It also holds The Great Revolution in Pitcairn, in which an American stranger lands on the island and talks its people into a revolution, with results Twain enjoys. The satire is aimed at the stranger and at the island’s admiring visitors, not at the islanders.
