Aurora Leigh

by Elizabeth Barrett Browning

A novel in blank verse, of 1856, about a woman who means to be a poet and will not be talked out of it. Emily Dickinson kept a portrait of Mrs Browning on her bedroom wall, and wrote more than one poem on her death. It is long, argumentative and alive.
