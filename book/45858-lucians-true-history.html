Lucian’s True History

by Lucian

Lucian wrote it in the second century as a parody of travellers’ tales, and begins by warning the reader that everything in it is a lie. His ship is carried by a whirlwind to the Moon, where the kings of the Moon and the Sun are at war, and later swallowed with its crew by a whale nearly two hundred miles long. Rabelais, Cyrano de Bergerac and Swift all learned from it.
