Forgotten Danger

by Joseph Samachson

A scientific experiment goes wrong in exactly the way early pulp fiction always assumed such experiments eventually would. I have been unable to find much else about either the story or its author beyond this one surviving text. It sat unread long enough that even I had forgotten precisely where it was shelved.
