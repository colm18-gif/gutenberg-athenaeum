War and Peace

by Leo Tolstoy

An entire country is invaded, several hundred characters fall in and out of love with each other regardless, and history keeps happening whether or not anyone in the drawing room notices. Tolstoy believed great men take credit for currents they do not actually steer. I keep the second half a shelf apart, for readers who need a rest between volumes.
