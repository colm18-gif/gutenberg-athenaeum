The North Pole

by Robert E. Peary

Robert Peary’s own account of the expedition of 1908 and 1909, on which he claimed to have reached the North Pole on 6 April 1909, with Matthew Henson and four Inuit men, Ootah, Egingwah, Seegloo and Ooqueah. Whether they got there has been argued ever since. The book is his case.
