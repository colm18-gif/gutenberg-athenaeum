The Hungry Stones and Other Stories

by Rabindranath Tagore

Tagore’s stories in English, among them the title story of a deserted palace whose marble seems to hunger for the people who once lived there. It is one of his few tales of the supernatural. The others are about ordinary Bengali lives, and are no less haunting.
