Stray Leaves from an Arctic Journal

by Sherard Osborn

Sherard Osborn commanded the steam tender Pioneer in the search for Franklin of 1850 and 1851, one of many ships sent north to look. His journal is lively, opinionated and full of the ice. Its full title calls it Eighteen Months in the Polar Regions.
