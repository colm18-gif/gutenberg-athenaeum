The History of the Island of Dominica

by Thomas Atwood

Dominica lies south of Montserrat, beyond Guadeloupe, and is the only other island where the mountain chicken still lives. Thomas Atwood, who had been chief judge there, published this history in 1791: its climate, mountains and rivers, its trade, its laws and its people. He wrote as an official of a slave colony, and the enslaved and the island’s first people appear only as he saw them.
