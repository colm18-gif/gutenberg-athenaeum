Making Fermented Pickles

by Edwin LeFevre

A United States Department of Agriculture bulletin on making sauerkraut, dill pickles and other pickles at home by salting vegetables and letting fermentation do the rest. It is short, practical and a little stern about clean crocks. Modern food-safety advice should be checked before following it.
