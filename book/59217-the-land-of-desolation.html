The Land of Desolation

by Isaac I. Hayes

Isaac Hayes had been to the far north twice, with Kane and on his own, before he went back to Greenland in 1869 as a passenger on a painters’ expedition. This is his account of that summer, of the fjords, the glaciers and the settlements. It is a gentler book than his earlier ones.
