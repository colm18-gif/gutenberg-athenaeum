Around the World in Eighty Days(eBook #103)

by Jules Verne

A meticulously punctual Englishman wagers his entire fortune that he can circle the globe in exactly eighty days, and then does, mostly through excellent train schedules and one loyal servant’s improvisation. Verne wrote an adventure novel that is secretly also a love letter to the timetable. It remains the most cheerful bet in this catalogue.
