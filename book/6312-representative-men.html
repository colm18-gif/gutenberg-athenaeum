Representative Men

by Ralph Waldo Emerson

Emerson’s lectures on six great men, Plato, Swedenborg, Montaigne, Shakespeare, Napoleon and Goethe, published in 1850. He gave them in Boston while Thoreau was living at the pond, and again in England in 1847 and 1848. While he was away, Thoreau kept house for his family.
