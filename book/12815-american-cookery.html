Food History

American Cookery

by Amelia Simmons

Printed in Hartford in 1796, this is the first known cookbook by an American published in the United States, by Amelia Simmons, an American orphan, as the title page says. It cooks with American things: corn meal, pumpkins, cranberries, and pearl ash to raise a dough. Its Indian pudding and johnny cake are written for American kitchens, not copied from English ones.
