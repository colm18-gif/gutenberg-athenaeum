Henry Esmond; The English Humourists; The Four Georges

by William Makepeace Thackeray

Thackeray gave his lectures on the English humourists of the eighteenth century in London in 1851, and began with Swift, of whom he drew an admiring and unforgiving portrait. This volume binds the lectures with Henry Esmond and with The Four Georges. Henry Esmond, his novel of Queen Anne’s reign, came out the year after the lectures.
