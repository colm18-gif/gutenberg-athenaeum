Punicorum Libri Septemdecim

by Silius Italicus

The longest poem to survive in Latin: seventeen books on Hannibal’s war with Rome, from Saguntum to Zama. Silius Italicus was a consul who retired to write it, owned the site of Virgil’s tomb and kept his birthday. Pliny said he wrote with more care than talent.
