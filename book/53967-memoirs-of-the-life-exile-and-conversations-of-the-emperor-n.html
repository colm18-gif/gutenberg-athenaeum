Memoirs of the Life, Exile, and Conversations of the Emperor Napoleon

by Comte de Las Cases

Emmanuel de Las Cases went with Napoleon into exile and lived at Longwood with him for more than a year, taking down what he said day by day, until the governor, Sir Hudson Lowe, had him sent away at the end of 1816. His record was published in 1823 as the Mémorial de Sainte-Hélène, and it did more than any other book to make the legend of the captive emperor on his rock. This is the first volume of the English translation.
