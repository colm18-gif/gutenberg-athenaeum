Mercy Philbrick’s Choice

by Helen Hunt Jackson

A novel of 1876, published without its author’s name in the No Name Series, about a young poet in a New England town. It has often been supposed that Jackson had her Amherst friend partly in mind. Two years later she persuaded Dickinson to let one poem, Success, go into another book in the same series, also unsigned.
