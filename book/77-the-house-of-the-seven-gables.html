The House of the Seven Gables

by Nathaniel Hawthorne

A family’s fortune and misfortune both trace back to a curse laid on the house generations earlier by a man his ancestor had wronged. Hawthorne believed inherited guilt does not politely wait for the guilty party to actually be present to collect. The house itself is easily as important a character as anyone living inside it.
