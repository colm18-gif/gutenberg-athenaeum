Poems

by Katherine Mansfield

Katherine Mansfield’s poems, gathered by John Middleton Murry and published in 1923, the year she died. Some are about her brother Leslie, killed in France in 1915, whose death turned her back to the New Zealand of their childhood. They are the margin of her work, and the margin is worth reading.
