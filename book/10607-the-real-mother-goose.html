The Real Mother Goose

by Blanche Fisher Wright (ill.)

Blanche Fisher Wright illustrated this collection of the old rhymes in 1916, and nearly every rhyme has its own picture: the cow over the moon, the old woman in her shoe, the four-and-twenty blackbirds. The rhymes are older than any book in the attic, and the youngest readers usually know them before they can read.
