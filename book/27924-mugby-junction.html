Mugby Junction

by Charles Dickens and collaborators

A junction offers more than one possible story. Dickens and his collaborators follow its branches into work, chance encounters, and uneasy warnings; The Signal-Man is among them. The depot is arranged on much the same principle, though I hope our signals are less alarming.
