The Chessmen of Mars

by Edgar Rice Burroughs

Princess Tara of Helium is blown off course in a storm and lands among the Kaldanes, living heads that ride about on headless bodies. Then there is jetan, Martian chess, played with living pieces and to the death. Burroughs printed the rules at the back of the book, so you can play it at home, more gently.
