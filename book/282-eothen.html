Eothen

by Alexander William Kinglake

Kinglake’s travels through the Ottoman Levant in the 1830s: Constantinople, the desert to Cairo, plague and Jerusalem. He deliberately left out anything improving and wrote only what struck him, which made it a model for modern travel writing.
