Voyage of H.M.S. Pandora

by Edward Edwards

The frigate Pandora was sent out in 1790 to bring the mutineers home. Captain Edwards took fourteen men at Tahiti and kept them in a cell built on deck, Pandora’s Box, and in 1791 the ship was wrecked on the Great Barrier Reef, where four of them drowned. This book prints Edwards’s report and the account of the ship’s surgeon, George Hamilton.
