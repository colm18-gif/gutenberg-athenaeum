Chitra

by Rabindranath Tagore

A play taken from a story in the Mahabharata, about the princess Chitrangada, who is raised as a warrior and falls in love with the hero Arjuna. Tagore wrote it in Bengali and put it into English himself. It asks, gravely and lightly, what it is to be loved for the wrong self.
