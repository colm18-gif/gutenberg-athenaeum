The Count of Monte Cristo

by Alexandre Dumas

A man is betrayed by his friends, buried alive in a fortress, and returns fourteen years later with a fortune and a very long list. He gets his revenge, methodically, and it costs him nearly everything revenge always costs. Dumas understood that patience, given enough time, is indistinguishable from a weapon.
