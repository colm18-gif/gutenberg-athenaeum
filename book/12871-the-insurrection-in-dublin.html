The Insurrection in Dublin

by James Stephens

James Stephens kept a record of Easter Week 1916 as it happened around him in Dublin: the shooting, the rumours, the burning of Sackville Street. It was published within months, before anyone knew how the story would end. It is the Rising seen from the pavement.
