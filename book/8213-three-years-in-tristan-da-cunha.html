Three Years in Tristan da Cunha

by K. M. Barrow

The one book on Gutenberg about the island itself. Katherine Barrow sailed in November 1905 with her husband, the Reverend J. G. Barrow, who went out as the island’s missionary clergyman, and it took them nearly five months to get there; they left in 1909. The book is her letters and journal of those three years, with the families, the canvas boats, the potato patches and the long waits for a ship.
