Adrift in the Arctic Ice Pack

by Elisha Kent Kane

Elisha Kent Kane went north as the doctor of the first Grinnell expedition, an American search for Franklin, in 1850, and drifted with it through the winter in the pack. This is his account, cut and edited by Horace Kephart in 1915. Kane went back north in command three years later.
