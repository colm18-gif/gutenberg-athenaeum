John Redmond’s Last Years

by Stephen Gwynn

John Redmond was member of parliament for Waterford city from 1891 until his death in 1918, and led the Irish Parliamentary Party to the Home Rule Act of 1914. Stephen Gwynn, a colleague and a friend, wrote this account of his last years: the war, the Rising and the collapse of nearly everything he had worked for. Waterford returned his son William to the seat after him.
