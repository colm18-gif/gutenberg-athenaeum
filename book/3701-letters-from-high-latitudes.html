Letters from High Latitudes

by Lord Dufferin

In 1856 Lord Dufferin sailed his schooner Foam to Iceland, Jan Mayen and Spitsbergen, and wrote these letters home to his mother. They are funny, well observed and full of the pleasure of a young man going north. The book was a great success, and is still a good read.
