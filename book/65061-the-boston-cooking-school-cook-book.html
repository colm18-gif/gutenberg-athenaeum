The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book

by Fannie Merritt Farmer

Fannie Farmer ran the Boston Cooking School, and her book of 1896 asked for level measurements: a cupful meant a cup levelled off with a knife. It made the measuring cup and spoon the American way to cook. Her publisher doubted it would sell, so she paid for the first printing herself.
