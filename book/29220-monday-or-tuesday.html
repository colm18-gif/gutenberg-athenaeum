Monday or Tuesday

by Virginia Woolf

Eight short pieces, the only book of Woolf’s stories published in her lifetime, among them ‘Kew Gardens’ and ‘The Mark on the Wall’, where a whole afternoon of thinking starts from a small dark mark above the mantelpiece. Read it slowly; it rewards the evening.
