All About Coffee

by William H. Ukers

William Ukers edited the Tea and Coffee Trade Journal in New York, and in 1922 he gathered all he could find about coffee: its botany, its history, the coffee houses of London, Paris and Vienna, and the trade from plantation to cup. It runs to hundreds of pages and pictures. Its account of the plantations is told from the merchant’s side of the counter.
