The German Arctic Expedition of 1869-70

by Karl Koldewey

The second German North Polar Expedition sailed for east Greenland in 1869 in two ships. One, the Hansa, was crushed in the ice, and her crew lived for months on the floe in a house built of coal bricks, drifting south until they could take to the boats. They all came home.
