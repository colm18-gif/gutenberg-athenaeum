The Voyages of Captain Scott

by Charles Turley

Charles Turley’s retelling, for younger readers, of Scott’s two Antarctic expeditions, in the Discovery from 1901 and in the Terra Nova from 1910. It is drawn from Scott’s own Voyage of the Discovery and from his last journals. It is the gentlest way in to the books on either side of it.
