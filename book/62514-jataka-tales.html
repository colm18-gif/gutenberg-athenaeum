Jataka Tales

by Ellen C. Babbitt

Ellen Babbitt’s retelling for children of the Jataka stories, tales of the Buddha’s earlier lives, published in 1912. They are often told with animals, and each has a moral. The Buddha appears as a deer, a monkey or a king.
