The Mystery of the Blue Train

by Agatha Christie

An American heiress is murdered aboard the luxurious Blue Train from Calais to the Riviera, and her famous rubies are gone. Hercule Poirot happens to be travelling on the same train. Christie wrote it in a hard year and never much liked it, but the train is glorious.
