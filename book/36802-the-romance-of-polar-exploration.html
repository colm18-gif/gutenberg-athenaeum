The Romance of Polar Exploration

by G. Firth Scott

A popular history of exploration at both poles, “from the earliest time to the voyage of the Discovery,” as its title page has it, published in the Edwardian years. Its author, G. Firth Scott, also wrote The Romance of Australian Exploring and a novel, Colonial Born. It is a good companion to the first-hand accounts on the shelf beside it.
