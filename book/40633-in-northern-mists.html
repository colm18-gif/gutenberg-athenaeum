In Northern Mists

by Fridtjof Nansen

Fridtjof Nansen’s history of what the ancient and medieval world knew of the far north, from Pytheas to the Norse in Greenland and Vinland. It was translated by A. G. Chater in 1911. This is the first of its two volumes.
