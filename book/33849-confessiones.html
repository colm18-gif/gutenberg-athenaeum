Confessiones

by Augustine

Augustine tells God the story of his life: a stolen pear, a mistress, a son, a long search through philosophy and a child’s voice in a Milan garden saying “take up and read”. It is often called the first autobiography. “Give me chastity and continence, but not yet” is his.
