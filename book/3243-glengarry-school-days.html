Glengarry School Days

by Ralph Connor

Charles William Gordon, a Presbyterian minister in Winnipeg, wrote as Ralph Connor, and his novels of the West sold in their hundreds of thousands. This one goes back to his boyhood in Glengarry County, Ontario, settled by Highlanders, where his father was the minister. It is a book of the schoolhouse and the snow, of the masters who came and went, and of the boys they tried to keep in order.
