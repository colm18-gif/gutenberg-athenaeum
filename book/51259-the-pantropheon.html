The Pantropheon

by Alexis Soyer

Alexis Soyer was the most famous chef in Victorian London: cook at the Reform Club, builder of a soup kitchen in Dublin during the Famine, and later reformer of the army’s cooking in the Crimea. The Pantropheon of 1853 is his history of food from the earliest times. It is crammed with Greek and Roman learning, worn lightly.
