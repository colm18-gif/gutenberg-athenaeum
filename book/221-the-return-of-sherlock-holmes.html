The Return of Sherlock Holmes

by Arthur Conan Doyle

The detective returns from a fall that was meant to be the end of him, to the considerable annoyance of everyone who had already grieved. Doyle brought him back only because his readers refused to let the matter rest. It is rare, in this catalogue, for public demand to actually resurrect anyone.
