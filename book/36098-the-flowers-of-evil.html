The Flowers of Evil

by Charles Baudelaire

Baudelaire’s poems of Paris, love, decay and boredom, published in 1857. Within weeks he and his publishers were prosecuted for offending public morals, fined, and made to cut six poems. A French court did not lift the ban on those six until 1949.
