The Home and the World

by Rabindranath Tagore

A novel of 1916, set in Bengal during the swadeshi movement against British rule, told in turn by a husband, his wife and his friend. It looks hard at what nationalism does to the people who believe in it. Tagore was himself sympathetic and wary of it, as the book shows.
