Jane Austen, Her Life and Letters

by William Austen-Leigh and Richard Arthur Austen-Leigh

William Austen-Leigh, a great-nephew of Jane’s, and his nephew Richard Arthur wrote this life in 1913 from the family’s letters and papers. It was the fullest account of her for most of the twentieth century. Deirdre Le Faye revised it in 1989 as A Family Record.
