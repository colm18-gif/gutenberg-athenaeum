Babbitt

by Sinclair Lewis

George F. Babbitt sells real estate in the booming Midwestern city of Zenith, believes everything he is told to believe, and is quietly miserable. Sinclair Lewis’s novel of 1922 gave the language a word for a self-satisfied man of business. In 1930 Lewis became the first American to win the Nobel Prize in Literature.
