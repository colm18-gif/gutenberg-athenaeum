The Octopus : A Story of California

by Frank Norris

Wheat farmers and a railroad fight over who actually controls a harvest neither one grew with their own hands. Norris wanted to write the whole economic machinery of America as if it were a force of nature, indifferent to any single person inside it. It is bleaker than most of what shares its shelf, and deliberately so.
