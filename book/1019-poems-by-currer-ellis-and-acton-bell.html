The Brontë sisters’ first book, published at their own expense in 1846 under three invented names; it sold two copies in its first year. Emily Dickinson read the Brontës all her life. At her funeral Higginson read Emily Brontë’s No Coward Soul Is Mine, a poem she had loved.
