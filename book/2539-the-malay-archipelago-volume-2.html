The Malay Archipelago, Volume 2

by Alfred Russel Wallace

The second volume of Wallace’s eight years in the islands between Asia and Australia, which takes him to the Moluccas, New Guinea and the Aru Islands for the birds of paradise. It was from Ternate, in the Moluccas, that he sent Darwin the essay that hurried the Origin into print. The first volume is in the Map Room.
