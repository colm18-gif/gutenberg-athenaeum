Emile

by Jean-Jacques Rousseau

Rousseau’s treatise of 1762 on bringing up a child according to nature, the boy Emile learning from things rather than books. Its chapter on religion, the Creed of a Savoyard Priest, had it burned in Paris and in Geneva, and Rousseau fled to avoid arrest. It changed how Europe thought about childhood all the same.
