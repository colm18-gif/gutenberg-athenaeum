Vikram and the Vampire

by Richard F. Burton

Richard Burton’s English version of the Vetala tales, published in 1870 as Vikram and the Vampire, or Tales of Hindu Devilry. It is the version in which the story of the king and the vampire entered English. Burton, better known as the explorer, got the tales from Hindi and thought them rather fine.
