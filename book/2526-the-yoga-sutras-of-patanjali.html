The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali

by Patanjali

A short Sanskrit text of aphorisms on yoga, traditionally ascribed to Patanjali and composed in the early centuries of the common era. Its subject is the discipline of the mind, and what is now sold as yoga is a small part of it. It takes a few hours to read and a good deal longer to understand.
