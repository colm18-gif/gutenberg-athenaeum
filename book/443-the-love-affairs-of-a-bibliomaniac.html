The Love Affairs of a Bibliomaniac

by Eugene Field

A light, affectionate memoir of one collector’s lifelong, slightly ruinous devotion to acquiring books he did not strictly need. Field wrote it with the specific warmth of someone confessing a harmless vice to people who share it. I suspect several regular visitors to this room will recognise a great deal of themselves in it.
