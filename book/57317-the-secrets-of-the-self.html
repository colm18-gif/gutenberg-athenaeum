The Secrets of the Self

by Muhammad Iqbal

Iqbal’s Asrar-i-Khudi, a Persian poem of 1915, translated into English by Reynold Nicholson in 1920. Iqbal was a poet and philosopher of Lahore, and his thought later bore on the idea of Pakistan. The poem is about the self and what it can become.
