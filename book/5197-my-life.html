My Life

by Richard Wagner

A composer’s own account of his turbulent life, ambitions, and considerable self-regard, dictated late in his career for an audience he clearly hoped would be generous. Wagner was no more modest on the page than he was famously in person. Read it for the history; keep your own opinion of the man entirely separate.
