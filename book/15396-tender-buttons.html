Tender Buttons

by Gertrude Stein

Gertrude Stein’s book of 1914, in three parts, Objects, Food and Rooms, describes things by every word except the usual ones. The first piece is called “A Carafe, That Is a Blind Glass.” Readers have argued ever since about what it means, which may be the point.
