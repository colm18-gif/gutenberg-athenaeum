The Thirty-Nine Steps

by John Buchan

Richard Hannay is bored in London until a stranger dies in his flat, leaving a notebook full of code and a plot against the country. He spends the rest of the book running across the Scottish moors with half the spies in Europe behind him. Buchan called it a shocker; it is also the model for nearly every man-on-the-run thriller since.
