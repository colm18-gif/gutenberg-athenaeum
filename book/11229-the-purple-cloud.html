Fading Speculative

The Purple Cloud

by M. P. Shiel

A poison cloud kills nearly everyone on Earth, and the sole apparent survivor must decide what, if anything, is worth rebuilding entirely alone. Shiel wrote one of the earliest true accounts of the last man on Earth, equal parts grandeur and dread. Solitude, at this scale, turns out to be closer to a punishment than a freedom.
