A Manual of the Art of Bookbinding

by James B. Nicholson

Nicholson was a Philadelphia bookbinder, and his manual of 1856 covers forwarding, gilding and the other branches of the trade. It was written for working binders, and it shows. A good companion at the bench, and at the marbling tray.
