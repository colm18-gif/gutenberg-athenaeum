The Adventures of Buster Bear

by Thornton W. Burgess

Buster Bear comes to live in the Green Forest and learns to catch fish, while Little Joe Otter wonders who is stealing his. Burgess wrote hundreds of these gentle animal stories for children. The sentences are short and friendly, and you learn a little about real animals too.
