Farthest North

by Fridtjof Nansen

Fridtjof Nansen had the Fram built to be frozen into the ice and carried across the Polar Sea by its drift, and in 1893 he let her go. When she would not go far enough north, he and Hjalmar Johansen left her with dogs and kayaks, reached further north than anyone yet, and wintered in a stone hut on Franz Josef Land. This is the first volume; the second is beside it.
