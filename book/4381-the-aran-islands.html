The Aran Islands

by J. M. Synge

Synge’s account of four summers on the Aran Islands between 1898 and 1901, living with islanders, learning Irish and listening to their stories. Yeats had told him to go there and express a life that had never found expression. Many of the stories he heard turn up later in his plays.
