The Round Towers of Ireland

by Henry O’Brien

Henry O’Brien argued in 1834 that Ireland’s round towers were pre-Christian temples, built by the Tuatha Dé Danann for rites he was glad to describe. George Petrie showed in 1845 that they were the belfries of Christian monasteries, as Ardmore’s is. O’Brien died the year after his book came out, at twenty-seven; it is still read, for its nerve.
