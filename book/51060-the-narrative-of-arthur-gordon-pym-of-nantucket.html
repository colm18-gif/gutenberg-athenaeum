The Narrative of Arthur Gordon Pym of Nantucket

by Edgar Allan Poe

Poe’s only complete novel: a stowaway, a mutiny, shipwreck, cannibalism, and a voyage toward a South Pole that grows impossibly warm and white. It ends mid-sentence, and writers from Verne to Lovecraft felt obliged to continue it.
