The Life and Adventures of Peter Wilkins

by Robert Paltock

Peter Wilkins is wrecked in the far south, is carried through a cavern into a hidden land, and marries Youwarkee, one of a people who fly on wings of their own. Robert Paltock published it in 1750, one of the many voyages to imaginary countries written after Gulliver. Coleridge, Southey, Scott and Lamb all loved it.
