Eskimo Folk-Tales

by Knud Rasmussen

Knud Rasmussen was born in Greenland in 1879, grew up speaking Greenlandic, and spent his life travelling among the Inuit and writing down what they told him. These are tales he collected in Greenland, put into English by W. Worster in 1921. The title uses the word of its day; the stories are the Inuit’s own.
