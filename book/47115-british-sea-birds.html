British Sea Birds

by Charles Dixon

Charles Dixon’s book of 1896 on the sea-birds of the British coasts, with the cliffs where they nest and how they live. The puffin, the guillemot, the fulmar and the great skua, which the Faroese call the skúgvur, are all here. It is the sort of book a fowler or a visitor to the cliffs would have carried.
