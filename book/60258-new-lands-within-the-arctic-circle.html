New Lands within the Arctic Circle

by Julius Payer

The Austro-Hungarian Tegetthoff expedition of 1872 to 1874 was caught in the ice and drifted north, and in 1873 its men saw land nobody had known was there. They named it Franz Josef Land. Julius Payer, who led the sledge parties, wrote this account; he was also a painter of the Arctic.
