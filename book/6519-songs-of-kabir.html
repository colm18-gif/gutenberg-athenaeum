Songs of Kabir

by Kabir

Poems attributed to Kabir, a weaver and mystic of northern India in the fifteenth century, translated by Rabindranath Tagore with an introduction by Evelyn Underhill. Both Hindus and Muslims have claimed Kabir, and he belonged to neither. The poems are short and pointed.
