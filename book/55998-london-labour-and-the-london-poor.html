London Labour and the London Poor

by Henry Mayhew

Henry Mayhew interviewed the people of London’s streets for the Morning Chronicle, and this first volume, of 1851, is largely the street sellers: of fish and fruit, hot eels, pea soup, baked potatoes, coffee and much else. It is the food of those who went by the area railings, in their own words. Later volumes go further into the city’s poverty.
