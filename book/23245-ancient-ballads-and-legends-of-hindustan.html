Ancient Ballads and Legends of Hindustan

by Toru Dutt

Toru Dutt died in 1877 at twenty-one, and this book of her versions of Indian legends was published after her death with an introduction by Edmund Gosse. She wrote in English and French, and learned Sanskrit as well. She is a poet people read once and then keep in mind.
