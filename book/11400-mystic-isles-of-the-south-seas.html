Mystic Isles of the South Seas

by Frederick O’Brien

Frederick O’Brien, an American journalist, lived in Tahiti and wrote this account of it in 1921, after his White Shadows in the South Seas had made him famous. He writes with affection for the islands, and with anger at what the coming of Europeans had done to them. It is of its time, and so is its romance.
