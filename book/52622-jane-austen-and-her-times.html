Jane Austen and Her Times

by G. E. Mitton

Geraldine Mitton, who wrote guides to London with Walter Besant, set Austen’s novels here against the England they were written in. Her chapters go through manners, money, travel, dress, amusements and the church, with the novels and letters for her examples. It came out in 1905, with portraits and prints of the time.
