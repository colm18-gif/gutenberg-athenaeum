The Voyage of the Beagle

by Charles Darwin

Darwin’s account of five years aboard HMS Beagle: Patagonian fossils, Andean earthquakes, and the finches of the Galápagos that he did not yet understand. It is a young man’s travel book, full of delight, before it became the foundation of a theory.
