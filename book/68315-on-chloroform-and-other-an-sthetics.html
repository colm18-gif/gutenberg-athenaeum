On Chloroform and Other Anæsthetics

by John Snow

John Snow was finishing this book when he died in 1858, and his friend Benjamin Ward Richardson saw it through the press with a memoir of him. Snow was among the first doctors to give anaesthetics as his particular work, and he measured his doses with a care nobody had shown before. He gave chloroform to Queen Victoria at the births of two of her children.
