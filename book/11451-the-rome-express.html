The Rome Express

by Arthur Griffiths

A passenger is found dead in a sleeping compartment of the express from Rome to Paris, and everyone else in the carriage becomes a suspect. Griffiths was an army officer and an inspector of prisons before he wrote crime stories. This one appeared in 1896.
