The South Pole

by Roald Amundsen

Roald Amundsen and four companions reached the South Pole on 14 December 1911, with dog teams from the Bay of Whales, five weeks before Scott. This is his own account of the Fram’s expedition, translated into English by A. G. Chater in 1912. It has the brisk, practical tone of a man whose plan worked.
