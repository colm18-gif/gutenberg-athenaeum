A Treatise on Adulterations of Food, and Culinary Poisons

by Friedrich Accum

Friedrich Accum, a German chemist in London, published this in 1820 and named the tradesmen convicted of adulterating bread, beer, tea, pickles and sweets. Its title page showed a pot with snakes and a skull, and the words There is death in the pot. The book made him famous and made him enemies, and within two years he had left England after a scandal over pages cut from library books.
