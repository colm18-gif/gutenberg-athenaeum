Reveries of a Bachelor

by Donald Grant Mitchell

Published in 1850 under the name Ik Marvel: a bachelor by his fire, imagining the lives he might have had. It was one of the best-selling American books of its day, and the young Emily Dickinson and her friends delighted in it. It is sentimental, and knows it.
