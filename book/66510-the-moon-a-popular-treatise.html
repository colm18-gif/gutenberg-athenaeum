The Moon: A Popular Treatise

by Garrett Putman Serviss

Serviss surveys the Moon as early twentieth-century astronomy understood it: its visible plains and mountains, the arguments over its origin, and the persistent question of whether it ever supported life. Keep its period assumptions beside what later observation corrected.
