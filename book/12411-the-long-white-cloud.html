The Long White Cloud

by William Pember Reeves

A history of New Zealand by William Pember Reeves, the country’s Minister of Labour in the 1890s and the man behind its pioneering law on industrial arbitration. He wrote it in London in 1898, as New Zealand’s Agent-General there. The title is the usual translation of Aotearoa, the Māori name for the country.
