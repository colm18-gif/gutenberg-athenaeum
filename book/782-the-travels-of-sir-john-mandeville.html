The Travels of Sir John Mandeville

by John Mandeville

In the late Middle Ages this was one of the most popular books in Europe: a knight’s journey to Jerusalem, India and Cathay, past dog-headed men and the fountain of youth. Most of it was borrowed from other books, and the knight himself may never have existed. For two centuries it was read as a true account.
