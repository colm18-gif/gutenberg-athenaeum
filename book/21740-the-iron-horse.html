The Iron Horse

by R. M. Ballantyne

Ballantyne’s novel of 1871 about life on the early railway: engine drivers, stokers, signalmen and a runaway train. He liked to learn his subjects first-hand, and his affection for steam is on every page. A signal house seemed the only proper place for it.
