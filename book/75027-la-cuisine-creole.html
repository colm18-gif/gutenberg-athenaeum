La Cuisine Creole

by Lafcadio Hearn

Lafcadio Hearn was a newspaperman in New Orleans before he went to Japan, and in 1885 he gathered this book of the city’s Creole recipes, gumbos, jambalayas and pralines among them. It is one of the first cookbooks of New Orleans cooking. The kitchens it records were shaped by French, Spanish, African and Caribbean cooks.
