The Sceptical Chymist

by Robert Boyle

Robert Boyle was born at Lismore Castle in 1627, the fourteenth child of the Earl of Cork, and grew up to be one of the founders of experimental science. In The Sceptical Chymist (1661) he doubts, at length and in dialogue, that everything is made of four elements or of three principles, and asks for experiment instead. Boyle’s law, on the pressure of air, came out of the same years.
