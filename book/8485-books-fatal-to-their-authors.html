Books Fatal to Their Authors

by P. H. Ditchfield

Writers who were burned, imprisoned, exiled or ruined for what they wrote, from antiquity to the eighteenth century. Ditchfield, a country clergyman, collected their cases in 1895. The book is gentler than its subject.
