The Modern Housewife

by Alexis Soyer

Soyer’s book of 1849 for the middle-class household, written as letters and talk between two housewives, Hortense and Eloise. It goes from breakfast to dinner parties and from the nursery to the sickroom. It is cheerful, practical and not above advertising its author.
