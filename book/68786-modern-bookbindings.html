Modern Bookbindings

by Sarah Prideaux

Sarah Prideaux was one of the finest binders of the Arts and Crafts years, at a time when few women worked at the top of the craft. Here she writes about the design and decoration of bindings in her own day. Her opinions are firm, and usually earned.
