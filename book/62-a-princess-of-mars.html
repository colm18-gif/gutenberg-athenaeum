A Princess of Mars

by Edgar Rice Burroughs

A Confederate officer is transported, without much narrative ceremony, to Mars, where he discovers lower gravity has made him unexpectedly formidable in a fight. Burroughs wrote pure escapism and never pretended otherwise. Take it for exactly what it offers and it delivers generously.
