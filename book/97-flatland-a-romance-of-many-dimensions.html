Flatland: A Romance of Many Dimensions

by Edwin Abbott Abbott

Edwin Abbott Abbott, headmaster of the City of London School, published it in 1884 as the memoir of A Square, who lives in a world of two dimensions. A Sphere comes down from above and shows him a world he cannot picture, and when he tells the other Flatlanders about it he is locked up. It is a satire on Victorian England as much as a lesson in geometry.
