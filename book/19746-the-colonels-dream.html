The Colonel’s Dream

by Charles W. Chesnutt

A former Confederate officer who has made his fortune in New York goes home to a small North Carolina town meaning to set it right with a cotton mill and good intentions. The town has other ideas. It was the last of Chesnutt’s novels to be published in his lifetime, in 1905.
