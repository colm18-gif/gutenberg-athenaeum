Voyager’s Tales

by Richard Hakluyt

Richard Hakluyt spent his life gathering accounts of English voyages, and published them in his great collection of navigations. This short selection lets readers dip into those accounts without taking on the whole collection. Hakluyt was a clergyman who sailed on none of the voyages himself.
