Carnacki, the Ghost Finder

by William Hope Hodgson

A detective of the supernatural invites four friends to dinner in Chelsea and, once the meal is over, tells them what he found. He brings an electric pentacle, a camera and a great deal of nerve. Some of the hauntings turn out to be human, which he reports with the same care.
