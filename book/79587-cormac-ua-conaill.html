Cormac Ua Conaill

by Patrick S. Dinneen

A historical novel in Irish by An tAthair Pádraig Ua Duinnín, Father Patrick Dinneen, best known for his great Irish-English dictionary. It was published in 1901. It was one of the first novels written in modern Irish.
