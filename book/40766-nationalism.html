Nationalism

by Rabindranath Tagore

Lectures given in Japan and the United States in 1916 and 1917, in which Tagore argues against the nation as an idea. He was no friend to the British Empire and no friend to rival nationalism either. It was an unpopular position and the book is still worth reading for that.
