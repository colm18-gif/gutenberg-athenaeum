Shackleton’s Last Voyage

by Frank Wild

Shackleton died aboard the Quest at South Georgia in January 1922, at the start of his last expedition, and Frank Wild took command and carried on. This is Wild’s account, published in 1923, with the expedition’s doctor, Alexander Macklin. The Quest arrived at Tristan da Cunha at dawn on 20 May 1922, and three chapters are about the island, with photographs of the Settlement and of John Glass and his family.
