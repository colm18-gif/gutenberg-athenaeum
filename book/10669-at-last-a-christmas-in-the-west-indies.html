At Last: A Christmas in the West Indies

by Charles Kingsley

Charles Kingsley, the author of The Water-Babies, had wanted to see the tropics since he was a boy, and the title is his cry when he did, in the winter of 1869. Most of the book is about Trinidad: its forests, birds and plants, described with a naturalist’s delight. His remarks about the island’s people are those of a Victorian clergyman, and some of them have not worn well.
