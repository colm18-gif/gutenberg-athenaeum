Narrative of an Expedition to the Shores of the Arctic Sea in 1846 and 1847

by John Rae

John Rae, an Orkneyman in the service of the Hudson’s Bay Company, wintered at Repulse Bay in 1846 and lived, as the Inuit did, off the land. This is his account of that expedition. In 1854 it was Rae who brought back from Inuit hunters the first news of what had become of Franklin’s men, and was abused in London for repeating it.
