Catriona

by Robert Louis Stevenson

A young man continues the adventure begun in an earlier volume, now navigating not war but the equally hazardous terrain of the woman he has fallen for. Stevenson gives the political intrigue a rest here in favour of a slower, more personal story. It is the quieter half of a two-part journey.
