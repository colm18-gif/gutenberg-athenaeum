A Doll’s House

by Henrik Ibsen

A wife walks out of her marriage, her children, and the entire arrangement of her domestic life in the final scene, closing a door that an entire era heard slam. Ibsen refused to soften the ending even under pressure to provide a happier one. The sound of that door has been argued about in theatres ever since.
