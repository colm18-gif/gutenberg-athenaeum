The Cricket on the Hearth

by Charles Dickens

The third of Dickens’s Christmas books, of 1845, subtitled A Fairy Tale of Home: John Peerybingle the carrier, his young wife Dot, and the cricket that chirps on their hearth as a household spirit. Down the road the toymaker Caleb Plummer tells his blind daughter Bertha that their bare room is beautiful, and the hard toy merchant Tackleton comes calling. There is no ghost in it, only a cricket and a misunderstanding, and it was soon being played on the London stage.
