True Tales of Arctic Heroism in the New World

by A. W. Greely

Adolphus Greely led the American expedition to Lady Franklin Bay of 1881 to 1884, and was one of only seven of its twenty-five men taken off alive, half-starved, at Cape Sabine. Later in life he wrote these short accounts of other people’s courage in the Arctic. He knew what he was writing about.
