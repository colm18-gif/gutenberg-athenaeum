Trent’s Last Case

by E. C. Bentley

An American financier is found shot in the garden of his English country house, and the painter and amateur detective Philip Trent solves it with dazzling logic. Bentley wrote it in 1913 partly to show that great detectives could be fallible. Say no more; the title is a clue.
