Seven Little Australians

by Ethel Turner

The seven unruly children of an army captain live in a rambling house beside the Parramatta River, and drive their stern father and young stepmother to distraction. Ethel Turner was in her early twenties when it was published in 1894, and it has been in print ever since. Readers who have finished it will know why it is not only a funny book.
