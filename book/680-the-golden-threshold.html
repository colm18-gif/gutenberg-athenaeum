The Golden Threshold

by Sarojini Naidu

The first book of poems by Sarojini Naidu, published in 1905 with an introduction by Arthur Symons. She later became a leader of the Indian National Congress and governor of the United Provinces. Gandhi called her the Nightingale of India, and this is where the name came from.
