An Illustrated History of Ireland

by Mary Frances Cusack

Mary Frances Cusack, the Nun of Kenmare, wrote this popular history in 1868. It tells the coming of the Normans: Strongbow took Waterford in August 1170 and married Aoife, Dermot MacMurrough’s daughter, there. Maclise painted the wedding, and his picture hangs in the room.
