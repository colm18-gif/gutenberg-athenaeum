Amphitryo, Asinaria, Aulularia, Bacchides, Captivi

by Plautus

Five comedies by Rome’s great comic playwright, among them the Pot of Gold, whose miser Molière borrowed for L’Avare. Plautus took Greek plots and filled them with Roman slaves, puns and songs. This edition prints the Latin opposite Paul Nixon’s English.
