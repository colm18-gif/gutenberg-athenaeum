The Kobzar of the Ukraine

by Taras Shevchenko

Shevchenko’s poems in English verse, translated by Alexander Jardine Hunter, a Scottish missionary doctor in Manitoba, and printed in Teulon in 1922. It was among the first books of Shevchenko in English, made for the children of Ukrainian settlers on the prairies and their neighbours. The reader can turn from it to the Kobzar in Ukrainian, on the racks of this room.
