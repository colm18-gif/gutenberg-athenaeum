The Wrong Box

by Robert Louis Stevenson

Two elderly brothers are the last survivors of a tontine, a lottery that pays out to whoever lives longest, and a railway accident sets off a comedy of mislaid bodies. Stevenson wrote it with his stepson, Lloyd Osbourne. It is odd and very funny, and not at all what you expect from the author of Treasure Island.
