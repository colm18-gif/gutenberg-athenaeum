Address to the Inhabitants of the Colonies, Established in New South Wales and Norfolk Island

by Richard Johnson

Richard Johnson came out with the First Fleet as chaplain to the convicts and the marines. In 1792 he wrote this address to the people of the two settlements, convicts, soldiers and free alike, urging them to keep the faith in a hard place, and it was printed in London in 1794. He dedicated it to all of them, “and especially to the unhappy prisoners and convicts”.
