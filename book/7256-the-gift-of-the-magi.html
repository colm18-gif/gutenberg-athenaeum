The Gift of the Magi

by O. Henry

A young couple in New York with one dollar and eighty-seven cents between them each secretly sells their most precious possession to buy the other a Christmas present. O. Henry is said to have written it in a few hours at a table in a New York tavern. The ending is famous, and it still lands.
