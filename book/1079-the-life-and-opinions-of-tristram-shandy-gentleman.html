The Life and Opinions of Tristram Shandy, Gentleman

by Laurence Sterne

The narrator sets out to tell the story of his life and does not manage to be born until the third volume. Sterne’s novel includes a black page, a marbled page and a great many digressions about noses. It is proof that experimental fiction is as old as the novel itself.
