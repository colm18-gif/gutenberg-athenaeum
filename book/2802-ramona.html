Ramona

by Helen Hunt Jackson

Helen Hunt Jackson was born in Amherst in 1830, the same year as Emily Dickinson, and knew her as a child. Ramona, of 1884, is her novel of the dispossession of the Mission Indians of southern California, written in the hope of doing for them what Uncle Tom’s Cabin had done against slavery. She was also the one friend who told Dickinson plainly that she was a great poet and ought to publish.
