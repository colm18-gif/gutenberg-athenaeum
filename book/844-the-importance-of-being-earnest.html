The Importance of Being Earnest

by Oscar Wilde

Two young men invent imaginary people to escape their duties, and both fall for women determined to marry someone called Ernest. Wilde called it “a trivial comedy for serious people”. It may be the most quotable play in English, and it reads in about an hour and a half.
