Some Experiences of an Irish R.M.

by Martin Ross and E. Œ. Somerville

Major Yeates, a retired officer, becomes a Resident Magistrate in West Cork and is outwitted at every turn by horse dealers, poachers and his landlord, Flurry Knox. Edith Somerville and her cousin Violet Martin, who wrote as Martin Ross, published the stories in 1899. They are among the funniest in the library.
