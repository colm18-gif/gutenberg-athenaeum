Schwatka’s Search

by William H. Gilder

Frederick Schwatka’s American expedition of 1878 to 1880 went to King William Island to look for Franklin’s records, travelling as the Inuit did, by sledge and dog team and living off the country. William Gilder went as the New York Herald’s correspondent and wrote this account. Much of what they learned, they learned by asking the Inuit who had seen the last of the expedition.
