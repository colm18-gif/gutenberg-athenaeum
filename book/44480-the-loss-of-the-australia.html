The Loss of the Australia

by Adam Yule

The brig Australia sailed from Leith for Sydney in October 1840 with twenty-eight aboard, and on 29 December, in the South Atlantic some six hundred miles from the Cape of Good Hope, she caught fire. This is her captain’s account of the burning, of what the crew and passengers went through, and of their rescue, put into shape by a Dundee minister. It is the ocean road that ships bound for Australia took, in the latitudes of Tristan.
