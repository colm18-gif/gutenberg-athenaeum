The Big Bow Mystery

by Israel Zangwill

A man is found with his throat cut in a bedroom locked and bolted from the inside, in a lodging house in the East End of London. Zangwill’s short novel of 1891 is often called the first full-length locked-room mystery. The solution is outrageous, and entirely fair.
