The Uncalled

by Paul Laurence Dunbar

An orphan in a small Ohio town is brought up by a stern spinster who has decided he will be a preacher, whether or not he has been called. Dunbar’s first novel, of 1898. He was better known for his poems, and minded that readers wanted only the ones in dialect.
