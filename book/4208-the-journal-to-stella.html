The Journal to Stella

by Jonathan Swift

From 1710 to 1713, while he was in London in the thick of politics, Swift wrote almost every night to Esther Johnson, his Stella, and her companion Rebecca Dingley in Dublin. The letters mix the gossip of ministers with what he ate for dinner, and break into a private baby language of his own. They were never meant to be read by anyone else.
