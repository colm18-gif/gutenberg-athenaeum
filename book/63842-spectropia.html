Spectropia

by J. H. Brown

A book of “surprising spectral illusions” from 1864: stare at the coloured ghost on the page, then at a white wall, and the ghost appears there in the opposite colour. It was written to show that ghosts are a trick of the eye. Best read in a dark room, such as this one.
