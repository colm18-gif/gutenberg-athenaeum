The Story of Geographical Discovery

by Joseph Jacobs

Joseph Jacobs, better known for collecting English fairy tales, wrote this short history of how the world became known in 1899. It runs from the ancient world to the explorers of his own day, and the maps get larger as it goes. It is a good first book for this room, and a quick one.
