The Belgian Cookbook

by Mrs Brian Luck

Belgian refugees who came to Britain after the German invasion of 1914 sent in these recipes, and Mrs Brian Luck edited them in 1915. The book was sold to help them. It is a small record of a cuisine carried into exile.
