Sakoontala

by Kalidasa

Kalidasa’s Sanskrit play of the king who forgets the woman he married, translated by Monier Monier-Williams in 1855. Kalidasa lived about the fifth century and is the most celebrated of Sanskrit dramatists. Goethe praised the play, and it helped to make the Western world aware of Sanskrit literature.
