The Orkneyinga Saga

by Joseph Anderson

The saga of the earls of Orkney, written in Iceland about 1200, in the English translation of 1873 edited by Joseph Anderson. Orkney, Shetland and the Faroes were settled from Norway in the same centuries, and their stories cross. It is the nearest thing the islands of the north have to a history of themselves.
