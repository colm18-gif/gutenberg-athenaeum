The Formation of Vegetable Mould Through the Action of Worms

by Charles Darwin

Darwin’s last book, and in its day his best seller. He spent decades measuring how earthworms bury stones and ruins, and had the piano played to them to see whether they could hear. They could not, though they felt the vibration when their pot stood on the instrument.
