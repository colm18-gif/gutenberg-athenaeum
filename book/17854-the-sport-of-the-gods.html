Returning Voices

The Sport of the Gods

by Paul Laurence Dunbar

A family’s fortunes collapse under a false accusation, and their move north for a fresh start delivers considerably less freedom than the journey promised. Dunbar was already celebrated as a poet, and brings a poet’s ear for injustice to the prose here. The title’s promise turns out to be far crueler than it first sounds.
