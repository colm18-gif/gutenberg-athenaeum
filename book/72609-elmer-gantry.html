Elmer Gantry

by Sinclair Lewis

A hard-drinking college football player discovers he has a gift for preaching, and rises through American religion without believing a word of it. Sinclair Lewis published it in 1927, and Boston banned it. It was denounced from pulpits across the country, which helped it sell.
