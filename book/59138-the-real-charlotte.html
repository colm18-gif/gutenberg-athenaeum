The Real Charlotte

by Martin Ross and E. Œ. Somerville

Charlotte Mullen, plain, clever and ruthless, schemes for land and love in a small Irish town. Somerville and Ross published it in 1894, and it is their masterpiece. Many readers think it the finest Irish novel of the nineteenth century.
