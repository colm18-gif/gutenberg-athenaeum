Army Life in a Black Regiment

by Thomas Wentworth Higginson

Higginson was a Unitarian minister and an abolitionist, and in 1862 he became colonel of the First South Carolina Volunteers, a regiment of men who had been slaves. This is his account of it, published in 1870, with a chapter on the spirituals he heard sung in camp and wrote down. Her letters reached him there, signed “Your Gnome”.
