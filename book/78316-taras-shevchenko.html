Taras Shevchenko

by Clarence A. Manning

A life of Shevchenko for English readers by Clarence Manning of Columbia University, with his poems put into English along the way. Manning follows him from serfdom to the Academy of Arts, to exile as a private soldier, and home to be buried above the Dnipro. It stands beside the Kobzar of the Ukraine, as the life beside the poems.
