Poetry

Taras Shevchenko

by Clarence A. Manning

Clarence Manning of Columbia University wrote this for the Ukrainian National Association in 1945: four chapters on Shevchenko’s world, life, poetry and faith, then the poems in his English. Katerina, The Poplar, The Dream, The Caucasus and the Testament are all here. The reader goes from it to the Kobzar in Ukrainian, on the racks of this room.
