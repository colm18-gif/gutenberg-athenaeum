Antigua and the Antiguans, Volume 1

by Mrs. Lanaghan

Antigua is Montserrat’s nearest large neighbour, under thirty miles away to the north-east. This account of its history, towns, estates and people, Black and white, was published in London in 1844, ten years after emancipation, and is credited to Mrs Lanaghan, who had lived there. This is the first of its two volumes.
