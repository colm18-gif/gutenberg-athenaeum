Where Animals Talk

by Robert Hamill Nassau

Animal stories from the coast of Gabon and Equatorial Guinea, collected by Robert Hamill Nassau, an American missionary who spent more than forty years in West Africa. The tortoise, the leopard and the little antelope compete, cheat and argue. They were published in 1912.
