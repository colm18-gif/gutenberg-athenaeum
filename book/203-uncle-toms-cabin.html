Uncle Tom’s Cabin

by Harriet Beecher Stowe

A novel written to make an entire country feel, in the body, what it had mostly managed to consider only in the abstract. It is credited, not entirely as a compliment by everyone, with helping start a war. Read it as the document it actually is: furious, sentimental, and aimed with total precision at its moment.
