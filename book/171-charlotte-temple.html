Society

Charlotte Temple

by Mrs. Rowson

A young woman is seduced by false promises of marriage and abandoned to a country and a fate the era’s readers were meant to take as a moral warning. It was, in its day, wildly popular precisely because it let respectable readers feel scandalised on someone else’s behalf. Read past the moralising and there is real grief underneath it.
