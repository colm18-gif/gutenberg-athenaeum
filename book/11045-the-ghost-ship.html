Fading Supernatural

The Ghost Ship

by Richard Middleton

A derelict, unlit ship appears at the edge of a quiet fishing village, and its crew’s fate turns out to be considerably stranger than any ordinary wreck. Middleton wrote very little before his early death, and this story is largely what he is remembered for. It has the compressed, complete quality of something that got exactly the space it needed.
