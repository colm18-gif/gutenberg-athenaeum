The History of John Bull

by John Arbuthnot

John Arbuthnot, physician to Queen Anne and one of Swift’s dearest friends in the Scriblerus Club, wrote these pamphlets in 1712 to argue that England should make peace with France. England is John Bull, an honest, hot-tempered clothier ruined by a long lawsuit against Lewis Baboon, with his attorney Humphry Hocus taking the fees. The name stuck, and John Bull stood for England in cartoons for two centuries afterwards.
