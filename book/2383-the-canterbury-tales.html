The Canterbury Tales

by Geoffrey Chaucer

A company of pilgrims riding to Canterbury agrees to tell stories on the way, with a free supper as the prize. Knights, millers, nuns and the much-married Wife of Bath all speak in their own voices, some of them rudely. Read it aloud if you can; it was made to be heard.
