Sailing Alone Around the World

by Joshua Slocum

Joshua Slocum sailed his sloop the Spray round the world alone between 1895 and 1898, the first to do so, and wrote this account of it. On the last leg home he crossed the South Atlantic and called at St Helena and Ascension. It is one of the best-loved books ever written about the sea.
