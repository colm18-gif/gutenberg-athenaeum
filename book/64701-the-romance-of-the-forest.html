The Romance of the Forest

by Ann Radcliffe

Adeline, a girl handed over to strangers by the man she believes is her father, takes refuge with a family on the run in a ruined abbey in a French forest, and finds the manuscript of a prisoner. Ann Radcliffe published it in 1791, and it was her first great success. Three years later came The Mysteries of Udolpho, the book Catherine Morland cannot put down.
