Tales for Christmas Eve

by Rhoda Broughton

Five ghost stories of 1873, among them The Truth, the Whole Truth, and Nothing but the Truth, told in letters between two women about a house to let in London. Broughton was famous for novels thought rather daring. Her ghosts are as brisk as her heroines.
