A True & Exact History of the Island of Barbados

by Richard Ligon

Richard Ligon lived on Barbados from 1647 to 1650, when sugar was new there, and published this account in 1657, with a map and drawings of the island’s plants. He describes the indentured servants brought from the British Isles, the enslaved people brought from Africa, the sugar works and the food. It is the earliest full description of an English sugar island, and it does not look away from how the sugar was made.
