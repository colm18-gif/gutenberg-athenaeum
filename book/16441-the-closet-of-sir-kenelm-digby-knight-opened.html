The Closet of Sir Kenelm Digby Knight Opened

by Kenelm Digby

Kenelm Digby was a courtier, privateer, natural philosopher and Catholic exile, and his recipes were printed in 1669, four years after he died. More than a hundred of them are for mead, metheglin and other honeyed drinks, many with the name of the friend who gave them. This is Anne Macdonell’s edition of 1910.
