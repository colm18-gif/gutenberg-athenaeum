A Farewell to Arms

by Ernest Hemingway

An American ambulance officer on the Italian front, the retreat from Caporetto, and an English nurse called Catherine Barkley. When it ran as a serial in 1929, the Boston police banned the issues of Scribner’s Magazine that carried it. In Mussolini’s Italy it was banned for its account of the retreat.
