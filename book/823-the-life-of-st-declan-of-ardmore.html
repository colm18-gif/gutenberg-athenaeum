The Life of St. Declan of Ardmore

by Patrick Power

Ardmore’s own tradition makes Declan a saint of the Déise before Patrick ever came to Ireland, and the round tower and the ruined cathedral on the hill there stand where his monastery was. This is his Irish Life, edited and translated in 1914 by Canon Patrick Power, a Waterford priest who also wrote the place-names of the Decies. Its miracles are told as plain history, which is how its first readers took them.
