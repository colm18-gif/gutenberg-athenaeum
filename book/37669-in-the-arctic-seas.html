In the Arctic Seas

by Francis Leopold M’Clintock

Lady Franklin bought the little yacht Fox and sent Francis Leopold M’Clintock north in 1857, when the Admiralty had given up the search. In May 1859 his second-in-command, William Hobson, found in a cairn on King William Island the only written record of Franklin’s expedition ever found. This is M’Clintock’s account of the voyage, and of what the record said.
