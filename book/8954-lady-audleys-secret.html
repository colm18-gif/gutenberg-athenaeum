Lady Audley’s Secret

by Mary Elizabeth Braddon

A golden-haired governess marries a wealthy baronet, and his nephew begins to notice that a friend vanished while visiting the house. Braddon’s sensation novel was a scandalous bestseller in 1862. The secret is less shocking now than the question of why the heroine felt she had to keep it.
