The Garden Party, and Other Stories

by Katherine Mansfield

Mansfield’s stories of New Zealand and London, most of them brief enough to read before a candle burns down. In the title story a girl arranges lilies for a party on the day a neighbour dies. Mansfield rarely tells you how to feel; she shows you the moment when a character almost knows.
