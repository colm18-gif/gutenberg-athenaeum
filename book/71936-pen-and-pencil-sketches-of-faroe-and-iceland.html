Pen and Pencil Sketches of Faröe and Iceland

by Andrew James Symington

Andrew James Symington sailed from Leith on 20 July 1859 in the Danish mail-steamer Arcturus, by way of the Faroes to Iceland, and this account of the voyage came out in 1862 with engravings by W. J. Linton. In Thorshavn he found grass growing on the roofs, puffin broth over open turf fires, and people as kind as any he met. He was also angry, and says so, when a fellow passenger shot a gull from the deck for sport.
