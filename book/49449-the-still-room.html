The Still-Room

by Mrs Charles Roundell

The still-room was where a country house made and kept its preserves, cordials, home-made wines and simple remedies. This small book of the early 1900s gathers recipes for them, from jams and syrups to pot-pourri. Some of its remedies are of their time and are best read, not taken.
