The Adventures of Peregrine Pickle

by Tobias Smollett

Smollett’s second novel, of 1751: the adventures of a proud, spoiled and quick-witted young man on the Grand Tour and in London, among practical jokes, duels and debts. His godfather, Commodore Trunnion, lives ashore in a house kept like a ship. Inside it Smollett printed the Memoirs of a Lady of Quality, the story of a real woman, Lady Vane.
