Essays — Second Series

by Ralph Waldo Emerson

Emerson’s essays of 1844, among them The Poet, Experience, Character and Nature. Experience was written after his son Waldo died of scarlet fever, aged five, in January 1842. The same year the book came out, Emerson bought the land by Walden Pond on which Thoreau built his house.
