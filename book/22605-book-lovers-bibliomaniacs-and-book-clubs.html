Book-Lovers, Bibliomaniacs and Book Clubs

by Henry Howard Harper

A short book of 1904 on the people who collect books, the ones who cannot stop and the clubs they form to keep each other company. Harper was a collector himself and knew the type from the inside. It is written with indulgence.
