Across Iceland

by W. Bisiker

William Bisiker sailed from Leith in June 1900, by way of the Faroes, and crossed the middle of Iceland from the north-east to the south-west; his account came out in 1902, with notes on the plants collected on the way. The Faroes have a chapter of their own, from Thorshavn. It is the work of a careful amateur naturalist, and reads like one.
