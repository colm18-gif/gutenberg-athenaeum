Pensées

by Blaise Pascal

The notes Pascal left at his death in 1662 for a defence of Christianity he never finished, published afterwards as his Thoughts. Some run to a page and some to a line: “The heart has its reasons, which reason does not know.” Here too are the wager and the thinking reed.
