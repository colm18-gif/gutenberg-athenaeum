The Secret Garden

by Frances Hodgson Burnett

An unloved, unpleasant child is sent to a cold estate and finds a walled garden nobody has tended in a decade. Tending it turns out to tend her as well, and a bedridden cousin along with her. It is the rare book in which a garden is the actual protagonist and everyone concedes the point gracefully.
