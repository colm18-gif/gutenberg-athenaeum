Niamh

by Peter O’Leary

A novel in Irish by An tAthair Peadar Ua Laoghaire, the Cork priest whose Irish, the speech of Muskerry, became a model for learners. It is set in the time of Brian Boru and the Battle of Clontarf. It was published in 1907, when books in Irish were still rare.
