Ritchie's Fabulae Faciles: A First Latin Reader

by Francis Ritchie

The first Latin reader generations of schoolchildren met: Perseus, Hercules, Jason and Ulysses, in easy Latin that grows harder as it goes. Ritchie wrote it so that beginners could read a continuous story as soon as possible. This American edition adds notes and a vocabulary.
