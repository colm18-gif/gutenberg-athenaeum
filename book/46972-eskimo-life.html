Eskimo Life

by Fridtjof Nansen

After crossing the Greenland ice cap in 1888, Fridtjof Nansen missed the last ship home and spent the winter at Godthaab among the Greenlanders. This is his book about the people he lived with there. He admired them, and said so, and was hard on the Europeans who came to improve them.
