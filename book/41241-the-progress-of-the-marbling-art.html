The Progress of the Marbling Art

by Josef Halfer

Halfer, a German bookbinder, worked out how the colours on a marbling bath behave, and his method, with a size of carrageen moss, was widely taken up. This is the English translation of his account. Read it before trying the tray, or after the tray has had its way with you.
