The Manual of the Botany of the Northern United States

by Asa Gray

Gray’s Manual, first published in 1848, was the standard flora of the north-eastern states for generations: every wild plant from New England to Wisconsin, keyed and described. It is not a book to read through. It is the book to have on the table when something has come up in the meadow.
