Glimpses of Bengal

by Rabindranath Tagore

Selections from the letters Tagore wrote in the 1880s and 1890s, when he managed his family’s estates and lived much of the time on a houseboat on the rivers. They are full of water, villages and light. Many readers think they are the freshest thing he wrote.
