The Home of the Blizzard

by Douglas Mawson

Douglas Mawson led the Australasian Antarctic Expedition of 1911 to 1914, whose base at Cape Denison turned out to be one of the windiest places on earth. On a sledging journey begun in November 1912 both his companions died, Ninnis down a crevasse and Mertz on the way back, and Mawson came back alone. This is his own account, published in 1915.
