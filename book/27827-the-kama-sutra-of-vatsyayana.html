The Kama Sutra of Vatsyayana

by Vatsyayana

A very old manual on pleasure, mistaken by generations of English readers for something far racier than the sober treatise it actually is. Most of it concerns manners, household economy, and the discipline of attention. I keep it filed under customs rather than scandal, on principle.
