The Clockmaker

by Thomas Chandler Haliburton

Sam Slick of Slickville, a Yankee clock pedlar, travels Nova Scotia selling clocks by soft sawder and human natur’, and tells the Nova Scotians what is wrong with them. Thomas Chandler Haliburton, a Nova Scotia judge, first wrote the sketches for Joseph Howe’s newspaper, the Novascotian, in 1835. They were reprinted and pirated everywhere, and Sam Slick was soon famous on both sides of the Atlantic.
