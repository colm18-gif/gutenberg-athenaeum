Narrative of a Second Expedition to the Shores of the Polar Sea

by John Franklin

John Franklin’s second overland expedition, of 1825 to 1827, went down the Mackenzie River and mapped the Arctic coast either side of its mouth, with John Richardson. It was better planned than his first, and nobody starved. Twenty years later he sailed for the Passage in the Erebus and the Terror and did not come back.
