Trips to the Moon

by Lucian of Samosata

Lucian openly promises ingenious lies, then sends his travellers into a war between the kingdoms of the Sun and Moon. Written in antiquity, it parodies extravagant travel histories so effectively that it became an ancestor of science fiction without intending to found a genre.
