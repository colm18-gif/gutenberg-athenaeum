The Gamekeeper at Home

by Richard Jefferies

Richard Jefferies, a Wiltshire farmer’s son, describes the life of a gamekeeper on an English estate: his traps, his dogs, the poachers and the vermin on his gibbet. It is exact and unsentimental, the work of a man who knew every hedge. His After London, in which England goes back to forest, is elsewhere in the library.
