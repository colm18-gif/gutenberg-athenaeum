The Felon’s Track

by Michael Doheny

Michael Doheny was one of the Young Irelanders of 1848, and this is his own account of the rising that failed at Ballingarry, written in New York the next year after he had escaped. Meagher is all through it. It is a participant’s book, angry and partisan, and none the worse a source for that.
