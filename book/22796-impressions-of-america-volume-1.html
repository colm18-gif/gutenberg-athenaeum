Impressions of America, Volume 1

by Tyrone Power

Tyrone Power was born near Kilmacthomas in County Waterford in 1797 and made his name on the London stage in Irish comic parts. He toured America from 1833 to 1835, and these two volumes are his account of it, sharp on the theatres, the steamboats and the people. His great-grandson, also Tyrone Power, became a Hollywood star.
