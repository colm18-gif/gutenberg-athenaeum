Cicero's Orations

by Marcus Tullius Cicero

A Roman statesman’s public speeches against the men he considered enemies of the republic, delivered with a wit sharp enough to get him killed for it eventually. He knew the risk and made the speeches anyway. I admire that kind of courage more than I admire most of the actual arguments.
