On the Reception of the ‘Origin of Species’

by Thomas Henry Huxley

Huxley’s own account of how the Origin was received, written for Francis Darwin’s life of his father. He remembers his first thought on reading it, “How extremely stupid not to have thought of that!”, and the fights that followed. It is short, lively and entirely on one side.
