Poems from the Divan of Hafiz

by Gertrude Lowthian Bell

Hafiz lived in fourteenth-century Shiraz and wrote ghazals, short lyrics of wine, love and the beloved that may also be read as poems about God; Persians still open his Divan at random to find an answer to a question. Gertrude Bell, later the archaeologist and diplomat who helped to draw the borders of Iraq, published these translations in 1897, after a stay in Persia. They are still counted among the best in English.
