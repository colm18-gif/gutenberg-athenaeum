A Tangled Tale

by Lewis Carroll

Ten knots of story conceal mathematical puzzles, inviting the reader to untangle them rather than merely admire the string. Carroll keeps the playfulness and the work close together. Do not hurry to the answers: getting pleasantly lost is part of the design.
