The History of Mary Prince, a West Indian Slave

by Mary Prince

Mary Prince was born into slavery in Bermuda about 1788 and was sold to owners on Turks Island and then in Antigua, Montserrat’s neighbour, who brought her to London in 1828, where she left their house. She told her story to Susanna Strickland, who wrote it down, and the Anti-Slavery Society published it in 1831. It was the first account of a Black woman’s life to be published in Britain, and it went through three editions in its first year.
