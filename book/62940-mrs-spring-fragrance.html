Mrs. Spring Fragrance

by Sui Sin Far

Stories of Chinese Americans in Seattle and San Francisco, written by someone who knew both sides of every joke the white neighbours made. Sui Sin Far was the pen name of Edith Maude Eaton, born in England to an English father and a Chinese mother. The book appeared in 1912, two years before she died.
