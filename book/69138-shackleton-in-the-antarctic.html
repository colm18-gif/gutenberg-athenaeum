Shackleton in the Antarctic

by Ernest Shackleton

Shackleton’s own shorter telling of the British Antarctic Expedition of 1907 to 1909, in the Nimrod. In January 1909 he and three companions turned back 97 geographical miles from the South Pole, the farthest south anyone had then been. Members of the same expedition made the first ascent of Mount Erebus and reached the region of the South Magnetic Pole.
