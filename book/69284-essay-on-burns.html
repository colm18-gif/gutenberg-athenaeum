Essay on Burns

by Thomas Carlyle

Thomas Carlyle, a stonemason’s son from Ecclefechan in Dumfriesshire, wrote this essay in 1828 for the Edinburgh Review, as a review of John Gibson Lockhart’s life of the poet. It is less a review than a long argument about what Burns was and what Scotland had made of him. It was reprinted again and again as a school text, as it is here.
