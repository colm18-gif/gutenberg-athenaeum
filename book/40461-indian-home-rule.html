Indian Home Rule

by Mohandas K. Gandhi

Gandhi’s Hind Swaraj, written in Gujarati in 1909 on a ship from London to South Africa, in which he sets out his case against Western civilisation and for self-rule. It was banned in India when it appeared. He said in later life that he would not change a word.
