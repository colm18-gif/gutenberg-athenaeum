The Chimes

by Charles Dickens

The second of the Christmas books, of 1844, written in Genoa within the sound of its bells: Trotty Veck, a poor ticket-porter who waits for errands under a London church tower, comes to believe the newspapers that his kind are born bad. On New Year’s Eve the goblins of the bells show him what might come of it. Dickens came back to London that December to read it aloud to his friends at Forster’s rooms, and Macready, for one, wept.
