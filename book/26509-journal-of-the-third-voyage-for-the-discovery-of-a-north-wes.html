Journal of the Third Voyage for the Discovery of a North-West Passage

by William Edward Parry

Parry’s third voyage, of 1824 and 1825, in the Hecla and the Fury, went south down Prince Regent Inlet. The Fury was driven ashore and abandoned, and her stores were left on the beach. Years later those stores kept another expedition alive.
