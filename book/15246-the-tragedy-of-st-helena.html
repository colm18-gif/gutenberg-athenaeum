The Tragedy of St. Helena

by Walter Runciman

Napoleon was sent to St Helena after Waterloo in 1815, and died there in 1821. It is the reason Tristan, its nearest inhabited neighbour though some 2,400 kilometres away, was first garrisoned: so that nobody could use it to rescue him. This is a shipowner’s account of the captivity, published in 1911.
