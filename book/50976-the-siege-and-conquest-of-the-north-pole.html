The Siege and Conquest of the North Pole

by George Bryce

A history of the long search for the North Pole, from the first voyages to Peary’s claim, published in 1910. George Bryce was a Canadian historian, and the Hudson’s Bay Company and the northern coast of Canada have a good share of it. It was written while the argument over who got there first was still hot.
