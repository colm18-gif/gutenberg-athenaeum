The Book of Household Management

by Isabella Beeton

Isabella Beeton was twenty-five when this came out as one book in 1861, after appearing in monthly parts published by her husband. Its thousand-odd pages cover servants, the law and the sickroom as well as cookery, and many of its recipes came from other writers, Eliza Acton among them. She died at twenty-eight, and her name sold cookbooks for a century after.
