The Forme of Cury

by Samuel Pegge

About 1390 the master cooks of Richard II wrote down nearly two hundred recipes on a roll, and Samuel Pegge printed them in 1780 with a glossary. It is one of the earliest cookery books in English, full of spices, almonds and colour: saffron for gold, sandalwood for red. Cury is the old word for cookery.
