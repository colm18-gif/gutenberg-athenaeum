The Velveteen Rabbit

by Margery Williams

A rabbit sewn from velveteen asks the old Skin Horse in the nursery what it means to be Real, and finds out the long way, by being loved threadbare by a small boy. Short enough to read aloud at bedtime; it is usually the one reading who needs the handkerchief.
