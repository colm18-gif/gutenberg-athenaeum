Old Deccan Days

by Mary Frere

Fairy legends of southern India, published by Mary Frere in 1868, which she took down from her ayah, Anna Liberata de Souza, in Bombay. Frere’s father was governor of Bombay at the time. It is a book of stories told by a servant to the child of the house.
