The Language of Flowers

by Henrietta Dumont

A flower book of 1851, giving each flower its meaning and a handful of verses, of the kind found in many American parlours. Emily Dickinson sent flowers from her garden and conservatory to her neighbours, often with a poem tucked in. In her lifetime more people in Amherst knew her as a gardener than as a poet.
