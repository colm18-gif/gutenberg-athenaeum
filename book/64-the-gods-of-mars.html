The Gods of Mars

by Edgar Rice Burroughs

John Carter returns to Barsoom and lands in the Valley Dor, the Martian heaven, only to find it is a trap kept by a false religion. Burroughs’s second Mars book is his angriest and one of his most inventive: white apes, plant men and the black pirates of the south. It ends on a cliffhanger that kept its first readers waiting for months.
