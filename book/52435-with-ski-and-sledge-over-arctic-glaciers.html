With Ski & Sledge over Arctic Glaciers

by Martin Conway

Martin Conway was a mountaineer, and in 1896 he went to Spitsbergen to cross it, with skis and a sledge. This is his short account of the crossing. It is about snow, glaciers and the weather more than anything else.
