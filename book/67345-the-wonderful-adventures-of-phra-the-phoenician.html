by Edwin Lester Arnold

A Phoenician trader dies in ancient Britain and keeps waking again, centuries later, to fight at Hastings, in the French wars and under Elizabeth. He is a little smug about it. Arnold, the son of the poet Sir Edwin Arnold, published it in 1890.
