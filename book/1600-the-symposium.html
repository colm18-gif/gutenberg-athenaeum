Philosophy

The Symposium

by Plato

A dinner party of Athenian men each take a turn praising love, until one guest arrives late, uninvited, and considerably drunker than propriety allows, and delivers the most honest speech of the whole evening. Plato lets Socrates’ famous argument sit right alongside the far messier human testimony surrounding it. It is the rare philosophy text that remembers people are also, stubbornly, bodies at a party.
