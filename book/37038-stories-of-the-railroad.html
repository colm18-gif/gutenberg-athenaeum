Stories of the Railroad

by John A. Hill

Tales of American railroad men in the age of steam: runaway engines, snowbound trains and the rivalries of the roundhouse. John A. Hill drove locomotives himself before he became an editor, and later helped found the McGraw-Hill publishing house. He knew exactly what it felt like at the throttle.
