The Hampdenshire Wonder

by J. D. Beresford

A professional cricketer wishes for an extraordinary son, and gets one: a child with an enormous head and a mind that frightens the village. A journalist tells the story. It is one of the first novels about a child born beyond his species, published in 1911.
