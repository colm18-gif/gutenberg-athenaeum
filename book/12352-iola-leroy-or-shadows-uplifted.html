Returning Voices

Iola Leroy; Or, Shadows Uplifted

by Frances Ellen Watkins Harper

A woman separated from her family by slavery and later reunited with them uses that reunion to argue, forcefully, for the dignity and uplift of her whole community. Harper was already a prominent poet and activist before she wrote it, and the novel carries that public conviction on every page. It insists on hope without ever pretending the cost of it away.
