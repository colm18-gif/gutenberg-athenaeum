Memory

De Profundis

by Oscar Wilde

A long, aching letter written from prison to the man whose betrayal helped put him there, equal parts confession, accusation, and reluctant self-examination. Wilde wrote it in disgrace, stripped of nearly everything, including for a long time his own reputation. It is the rawest thing he ever published, and utterly unlike the wit he was famous for.
