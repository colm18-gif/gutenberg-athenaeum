Letters on Natural Magic

by David Brewster

Brewster, who invented the kaleidoscope, wrote these letters to Sir Walter Scott in 1832 to explain the wonders that had passed for magic: speaking statues, ghostly apparitions, tricks of mirrors and lenses. Each is taken apart and shown to be natural philosophy. The right book for a room that shows the library on a table.
