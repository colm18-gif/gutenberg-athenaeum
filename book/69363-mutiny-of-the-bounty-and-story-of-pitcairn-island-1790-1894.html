Mutiny of the Bounty and Story of Pitcairn Island, 1790-1894

by Rosalind Amelia Young

Rosalind Amelia Young was born on Pitcairn in 1853, a descendant of the mutineer Edward Young, and wrote this history of her island’s first hundred years. It tells the mutiny and the killings of the early years briefly, and then the island’s own life: its ships and visitors, the move to Norfolk Island in 1856 and the families who came back, its school and its church. Published in 1894, it is the only book on these shelves written by an islander.
