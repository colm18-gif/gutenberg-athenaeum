Daddy-Long-Legs

by Jean Webster

An orphan girl is sent to college by a rich man she has never met, on one condition: she must write to him every month. The whole book is her letters, full of jokes, little drawings and the new words she is learning. Letters are short, so it is easy to read a little at a time.
