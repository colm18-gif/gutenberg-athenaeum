A Description of Greenland

by Hans Egede

Hans Egede, a Norwegian pastor, sailed to Greenland in 1721 to find the descendants of the old Norse settlers, found the Inuit instead, and stayed fifteen years as a missionary. This is his description of the country, its people and its animals. His mission was the beginning of the Danish colony, and his book the first full account of Greenland for a European reader.
