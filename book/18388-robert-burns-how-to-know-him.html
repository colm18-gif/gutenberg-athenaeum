Robert Burns: How To Know Him

by William Allan Neilson

William Allan Neilson was born in Doune in Perthshire, taught English at Harvard, and became president of Smith College in 1917, the year this book came out. It is a reader’s guide, with the poems themselves set out at length between his chapters. He later edited the second edition of Webster’s New International Dictionary.
