Three Voyages for the Discovery of a Northwest Passage

by William Edward Parry

William Edward Parry took the Hecla and the Griper west through Lancaster Sound in 1819, further than anyone had gone, and wintered at Melville Island. His crews kept their spirits up through the dark with a school, a newspaper and plays. The first of his three voyages, told in his own words.
