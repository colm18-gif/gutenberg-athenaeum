The Librarian at Play

by Edmund Lester Pearson

Humorous sketches of library life from 1911, by a librarian who wrote a column called The Librarian for the Boston Transcript. Pearson also invented an eighteenth-century almanac for librarians and fooled a good many of them. Here he is mostly telling the truth.
