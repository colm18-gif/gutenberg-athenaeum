Stray Birds

by Rabindranath Tagore

Short, aphoristic verses published in 1916, many of them put into English by Tagore himself from his Bengali. They are the smallest things on this shelf and are often the ones people remember. A reader can finish one in the time it takes the lamp to flicker.
