Journey

The West Indies

by Nancy Prince

Nancy Prince, a free Black woman born in Massachusetts in 1799, sailed to Jamaica in 1840 to work among the people freed when slavery there ended two years before. This short book of 1841 describes the islands, the freedmen’s schools and chapels, and the hardships of the people she met. Its long title promises an account of the progress of Christianity, education and liberty among the coloured population, and it is plain, brief and first-hand.
