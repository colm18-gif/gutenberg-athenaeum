The Building of a Book

by Frederick H. Hitchcock

A book of 1906 in which experts describe, one chapter each, every stage of making a book, from the author’s manuscript to the bookseller’s counter. Hitchcock edited it for the trade. It is the library’s only manual for its own contents.
