Indian Fairy Tales

by Joseph Jacobs

Joseph Jacobs’s collection of Indian tales for children, published in 1892, drawn from the Jataka stories, the Panchatantra and the folklore of the villages. Jacobs collected English and Celtic fairy tales as well, and brought the same plain voice here. The tigers and jackals are cleverer than the kings.
