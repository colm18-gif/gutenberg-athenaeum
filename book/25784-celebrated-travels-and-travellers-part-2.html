Celebrated Travels and Travellers, Part 2.

by Jules Verne

Verne’s history of exploration, from the ancients and Marco Polo through Columbus, Cook and the navigators of the Pacific. He wrote it to know every coast his heroes might visit; it reads like a library of voyages in one volume. This is another English translation; the library keeps each one, because translators disagree in interesting ways.
