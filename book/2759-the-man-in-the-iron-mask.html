The Man in the Iron Mask

by Alexandre Dumas

The ageing musketeers discover the most dangerous secret in France: a prisoner in the Bastille whose face must never be seen, because it is the face of the king. Dumas built the story on a real prisoner of Louis XIV whose identity is still argued over. It is the last part of the musketeers’ long saga, and the saddest.
