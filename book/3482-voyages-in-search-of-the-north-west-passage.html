Voyages in Search of the North-West Passage

by Richard Hakluyt

The Elizabethan voyages that first went looking for a way round the top of America, taken from Richard Hakluyt’s great collection of English navigations. Henry Morley edited this small selection for Cassell’s National Library, for readers who would never open the folio. The ice was there from the beginning, and so was the hope.
