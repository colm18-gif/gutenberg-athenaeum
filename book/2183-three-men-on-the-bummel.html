Three Men on the Bummel

by Jerome K. Jerome

The three men from the boat, a little older now, take a cycling holiday through Germany, with trains, luggage and a great deal of confusion over the language. Jerome’s observations on the German love of rules are affectionate and very funny. Three Men in a Boat is also in this library, if you would like to start there.
