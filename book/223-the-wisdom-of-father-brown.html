The Wisdom of Father Brown

by G. K. Chesterton

More cases for the small, unassuming priest whose method remains disarmingly simple: understand the sinner well enough, and the crime explains itself. Chesterton keeps proving that humility is a sharper investigative tool than arrogance. I find him easier to trust than most fictional detectives, and I have met a great many.
