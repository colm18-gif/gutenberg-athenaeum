Vitruvii De architectura libri decem

by Vitruvius

Ten books on architecture by a Roman engineer of Augustus’s day: temples, town planning, materials, water, sundials and war machines. His rule that a building needs firmness, usefulness and beauty is still quoted in architecture schools. Leonardo’s drawing of a man in a circle and a square comes from his third book.
