Flatland: A Romance of Many Dimensions

by Edwin A. Abbott

A square living on a flat plane is visited by a sphere and must accept that his entire world is a single page. Abbott wrote it as mathematics and satire at once: the geometry is a lesson in dimensions, the social order of Flatland a lesson in who gets to decide what is real.
