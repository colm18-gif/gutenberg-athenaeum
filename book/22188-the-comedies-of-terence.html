The Comedies of Terence

by Terence

Publius Terentius Afer came to Rome from Carthage as a slave, was freed by his master, and wrote six comedies in the 160s BC, all of which survive. His surname, Afer, means the African. One of his lines is still quoted: I am human, and I think nothing human alien to me.
