Mrs. Warren’s Profession

by George Bernard Shaw

A clever young Cambridge graduate discovers how her mother paid for her education: by running brothels across Europe. Shaw wrote it in 1893 and the Lord Chamberlain banned it, so for decades London could see it only in private performances. In New York in 1905 the police arrested the cast after the first night.
