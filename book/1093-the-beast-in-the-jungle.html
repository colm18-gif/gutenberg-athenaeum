The Beast in the Jungle

by Henry James

John Marcher is sure that something rare and terrible is waiting to happen to him, and a woman agrees to wait for it beside him, for years. James’s long story is about a life spent waiting for life to begin. Its last scene, in a cemetery, is one of the most painful he ever wrote.
