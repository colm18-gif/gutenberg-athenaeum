The Leavenworth Case

by Anna Katharine Green

A wealthy New York merchant is found shot in his library, and suspicion falls on one of his two beautiful nieces. Published in 1878, nine years before Sherlock Holmes, it was one of the first detective novels written by a woman, and a huge bestseller. Agatha Christie read it as a girl and never forgot it.
