Rights of Man

by Thomas Paine

Paine’s answer to Edmund Burke’s attack on the French Revolution, published in two parts in 1791 and 1792: governments exist for the people, and the people may change them. It sold in enormous numbers. Paine was tried for seditious libel and convicted in his absence, having already left for France.
