The Sorcery Club

by Elliott O’Donnell

A circle of ordinary Londoners discover they can actually summon what their society meetings previously only pretended to summon, with consequences exactly as disastrous as you would expect. O’Donnell wrote extensively on the supernatural as though it were simply an underreported branch of natural history. He was, by all accounts, entirely sincere about that.
