Two Years Before the Mast

by Richard Henry Dana Jr.

A Harvard student with weak eyes signed on as a common sailor round Cape Horn to California in 1834. Dana’s account of flogging, hides and gales changed how Americans saw life before the mast, and Melville admired it deeply.
