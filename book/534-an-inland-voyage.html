An Inland Voyage

by Robert Louis Stevenson

A travel memoir of a canoe trip through Belgium and France, written by a young man who had not yet become the novelist everyone now remembers him as. It is slight, cheerful, and entirely unbothered by its own smallness. Every great career starts somewhere modest; this is where his began.
