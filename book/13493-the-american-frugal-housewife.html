The American Frugal Housewife

by Lydia Maria Child

Lydia Maria Child’s book for households of small means, first published in Boston in 1829 as The Frugal Housewife and soon renamed, since an English book already had the title. Its whole argument is thrift: nothing wasted, nothing bought that can be made at home. Child was also a novelist and an abolitionist, and her Appeal in Favor of That Class of Americans Called Africans of 1833 cost her much of her readership.
