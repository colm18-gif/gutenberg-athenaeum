Akbar, Emperor of India

by Richard von Garbe

Richard von Garbe’s short life of the Mughal emperor Akbar, published in 1909. Akbar ruled from 1556 to 1605 and was known for his interest in all religions. Garbe was a German Sanskritist, and the book reflects his admiration.
