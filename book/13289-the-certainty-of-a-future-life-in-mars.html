The Certainty of a Future Life in Mars

by L. P. Gratacap

A father promises his son that after death he will send word from Mars, where he believes souls go next, and the messages arrive by wireless. Louis Pope Gratacap was a curator at the American Museum of Natural History. It is strange and earnest, and it is the reason the wireless set in the ruin is kept switched on.
