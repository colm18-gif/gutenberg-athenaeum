The Tale of Peter Rabbit

by Beatrix Potter

Peter is told, very clearly, not to go into Mr. McGregor’s garden, and goes straight there. He loses his jacket and his shoes and very nearly a good deal more, and ends the day with camomile tea while his sisters have blackberries. Five minutes long, and still quietly at work on disobedient children.
