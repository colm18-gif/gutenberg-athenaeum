Carmilla

by J. Sheridan Le Fanu

A lonely girl in a Styrian castle welcomes a mysterious guest, Carmilla, whose affection grows alarming. Le Fanu’s vampire story came twenty-five years before Dracula and is subtler: more about longing and illness than fangs. The portrait dated 1698 is the detail everyone remembers.
