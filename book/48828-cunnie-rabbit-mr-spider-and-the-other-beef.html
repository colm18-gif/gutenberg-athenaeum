Cunnie Rabbit, Mr. Spider and the Other Beef

by Florence M. Cronise

Animal stories from Sierra Leone, gathered by Florence Cronise and Henry Ward and published in 1903. Cunnie Rabbit is small and clever, and Mr Spider greedy and full of tricks. Readers who know Brer Rabbit will recognise some of the plots, which travelled to America with enslaved Africans.
