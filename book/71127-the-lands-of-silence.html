The Lands of Silence

by Clements R. Markham

Clements Markham had sailed in the search for Franklin as a young man, and later, as president of the Royal Geographical Society, sent Scott south. This history of exploration at both poles was his last book, published after his death in 1916. It is a lifetime’s reading, and its opinions are firmly held.
