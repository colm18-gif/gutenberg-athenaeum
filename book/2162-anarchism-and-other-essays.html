Anarchism and Other Essays

by Emma Goldman

Emma Goldman’s essays of 1910 on anarchism, prisons, patriotism, marriage and the traffic in women. She was jailed more than once for what she said in public, about birth control and against the draft among other things. In 1919 the United States deported her to Russia, which she found no freer.
