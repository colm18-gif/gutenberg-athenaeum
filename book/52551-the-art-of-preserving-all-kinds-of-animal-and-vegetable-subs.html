The Art of Preserving All Kinds of Animal and Vegetable Substances

by Nicolas Appert

Nicolas Appert, a Paris confectioner, found that food sealed in glass bottles and heated in boiling water would keep for years, and in 1810 the French government gave him a prize for publishing how. He did not know why it worked; that had to wait for Pasteur. This is the English translation of his second edition.
