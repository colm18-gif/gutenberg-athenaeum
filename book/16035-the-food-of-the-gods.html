The Food of the Gods

by Brandon Head

A popular account of cocoa and chocolate, from the Aztec cup to the Victorian factory. The title comes from Linnaeus’s name for the cocoa tree, Theobroma, food of the gods. It is a short book, and as fond of its subject as the title suggests.
