Of One Blood

by Pauline E. Hopkins

A Harvard medical student who has kept his ancestry secret joins an expedition to Ethiopia and finds a hidden city that has been waiting for its king. Pauline Hopkins serialised it in the Colored American Magazine, which she edited, in 1902 and 1903. It was not published as a book until 1988.
