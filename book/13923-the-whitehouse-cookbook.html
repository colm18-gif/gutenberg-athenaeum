The Whitehouse Cookbook

by F. L. Gillette

Fanny Lemira Gillette wrote this in 1887 with Hugo Ziemann, who had been steward of the White House, and the title promised the President’s table. It is a large American household book, with menus, table manners, the toilet and the sickroom as well as recipes. It stayed in print, revised, for decades.
