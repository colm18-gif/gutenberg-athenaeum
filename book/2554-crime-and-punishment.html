Conscience

Crime and Punishment

by Fyodor Dostoyevsky

A student commits a murder he has already justified to himself in an essay, then spends four hundred pages failing to enjoy having gotten away with it. Guilt, this book argues, is not a moral failure but a kind of terrible honesty. Read it slowly; it was not built for skimming.
