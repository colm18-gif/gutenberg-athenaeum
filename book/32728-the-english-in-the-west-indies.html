The English in the West Indies

by James Anthony Froude

Froude, one of the best-known historians of his day, toured the British West Indies in the winter of 1886 and argued in this book of 1888 that their Black majority should never be given self-government. It was answered within a year by J. J. Thomas’s Froudacity, on the shelf across the room. It stands here because it was answered, and because it shows how the islands were written about.
