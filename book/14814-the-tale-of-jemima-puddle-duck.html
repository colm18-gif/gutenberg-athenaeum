The Tale of Jemima Puddle-Duck

by Beatrix Potter

Jemima wants to hatch her own eggs and goes off to find a safe place for a nest, in her shawl and poke bonnet. A charming gentleman with sandy whiskers offers his woodshed and asks her to bring the herbs for an omelette. The collie, Kep, understands the situation sooner than she does.
