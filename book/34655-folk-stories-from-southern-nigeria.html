Folk Stories from Southern Nigeria

by Elphinstone Dayrell

Stories from the Cross River country of southern Nigeria, written down by Elphinstone Dayrell, a colonial district officer, and published in 1910 with an introduction by Andrew Lang. There are tortoises, leopards, kings and ghosts, and explanations of how things came to be. They read like stories meant to be told aloud.
