The Three Musketeers

by Alexandre Dumas

Three sworn companions and the young man determined to join them get tangled in court intrigue, secret alliances, and one especially dangerous woman who outmanoeuvres nearly everyone in the book. Dumas wrote adventure with such infectious momentum that the plot barely pauses for you to catch your breath. Loyalty, in this one, is treated as a discipline rather than simply a feeling.
