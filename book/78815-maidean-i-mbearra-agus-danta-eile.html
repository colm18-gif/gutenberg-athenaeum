Maidean i mBéarra agus dánta eile

by Osborn Bergin

Poems in Irish by Osborn Bergin, the Cork-born scholar of Old and Middle Irish. The title poem is a morning in Béarra, the peninsula in west Cork and Kerry. Scholars knew him for editing the poems of others; these are his own.
