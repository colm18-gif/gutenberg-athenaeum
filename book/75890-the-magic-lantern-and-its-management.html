The Magic Lantern and Its Management

by Thomas Cradock Hepworth

A practical handbook to the magic lantern, the projector of Victorian lectures and entertainments: lamps, slides, screens, and how not to set fire to any of them. It is written for working lanternists. The camera obscura brings the world in; the lantern sends pictures out.
