Returning Voices

The House Behind the Cedars

by Charles W. Chesnutt

A man who can pass as white chooses, deliberately and at real personal cost, to live openly as Black instead, and the book follows everyone in his family who makes a different choice. Chesnutt uses one household to examine every possible answer to the same impossible question. No single choice in it is treated as simply right or simply wrong.
