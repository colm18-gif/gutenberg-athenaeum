Anne of Green Gables

by L. M. Montgomery

An orphan arrives expecting to be useful on a farm and is instead, through sheer persistent imagination, nearly impossible to contain. She talks constantly, apologises rarely, and wins over an entire village by simply refusing to be quiet about her own inner life. I have met several visitors to this library who claim her as a personal influence, unprompted.
