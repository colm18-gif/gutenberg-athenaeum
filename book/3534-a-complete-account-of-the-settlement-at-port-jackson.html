A Complete Account of the Settlement at Port Jackson

by Watkin Tench

Watkin Tench, a captain of marines, wrote the best account of the colony’s first hungry years, published in 1793. In it the news comes to Port Jackson in April 1790 that the Sirius has been lost at Norfolk Island, with the colony already short of food. Tench writes plainly and well, and with sympathy for nearly everyone.
