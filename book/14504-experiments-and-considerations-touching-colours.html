Experiments and Considerations Touching Colours

by Robert Boyle

Boyle’s book of 1664 gathers what he had seen in prisms, tinctures and polished metals, and argues that colour is a matter of how light is changed by the surfaces it meets. Isaac Newton read it as a young man. Lismore’s most famous son wrote it at Oxford, where he did most of his work.
