The Curiosities of Food

by P. L. Simmonds

Peter Lund Simmonds wrote on trade and the produce of the empire, and in 1859 he gathered what the world ate from the animal kingdom: birds’ nests, insects, snails, sea slugs and stranger dishes. It is a Victorian survey and sees the world from London. It is also a reminder that one table’s curiosity is another’s supper.
