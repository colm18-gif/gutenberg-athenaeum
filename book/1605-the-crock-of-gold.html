The Crock of Gold

by James Stephens

Two philosophers in a pine wood, a leprechaun’s stolen pot of gold, a policeman and the god Pan all find their way into the same story, and so, in the end, does Angus Óg. James Stephens published it in 1912, and it won the Polignac Prize. It is comic, wise and entirely its own.
