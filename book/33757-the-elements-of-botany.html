The Elements of Botany

by Asa Gray

Asa Gray of Harvard was the leading American botanist of the century, and Darwin’s correspondent and champion in the United States. This is his book for beginners and schools. Emily Dickinson studied botany at Amherst Academy and at Mount Holyoke, and pressed more than four hundred plants into an album, each with its Latin name.
