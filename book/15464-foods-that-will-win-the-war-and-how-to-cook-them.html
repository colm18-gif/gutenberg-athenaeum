Foods That Will Win the War and How to Cook Them

by C. Houston Goudiss

Published in 1918 by C. Houston and Alberta M. Goudiss, this helped American households answer the Food Administration’s call to save wheat, meat, fat and sugar for the troops and the Allies. Its recipes use corn meal, barley, potatoes and syrups instead. It is a cookbook as war work, with wheatless and meatless days.
