A Narrative of the Mutiny, on Board His Majesty’s Ship Bounty

by William Bligh

Bligh’s own account, published in 1790 within months of his return: the mutiny of 28 April 1789, and his voyage of more than three thousand six hundred miles in the ship’s open launch to Timor, with eighteen men and very little food. Only one of them died on the way, killed by islanders on Tofua. It is short, exact and angry.
