The Eventful History of the Mutiny and Piratical Seizure of H.M.S. Bounty

by John Barrow

Sir John Barrow, Second Secretary to the Admiralty, published this account in 1831, drawing on the papers the Admiralty held. It tells the voyage, the mutiny, the court martial of the men brought back from Tahiti, and the finding of Pitcairn and its last mutineer. It was the standard history for a century, and it fed the long argument over whether Bligh or Christian was more to blame.
