Hindoo Tales

by Dandin

A Victorian translation of the Sanskrit Dasakumaracharita, the adventures of ten princes, by the seventh-century writer Dandin. It is a picaresque, full of disguises, thieves and escapes. Nobody could call it a children’s book.
