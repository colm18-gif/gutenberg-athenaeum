The English Housekeeper

by Anne Cobbett

Anne Cobbett, daughter of the radical William Cobbett, wrote this manual of domestic management in the 1830s. It takes in the servants, marketing, carving and the store-room as well as the cooking. It is brisk, careful with money and severe about waste.
