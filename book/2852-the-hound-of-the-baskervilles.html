The Hound of the Baskervilles

by Arthur Conan Doyle

A legend of a spectral hound stalking a family’s moor estate draws a detective out of London and into considerably wilder country than his usual beat. It is the most atmospheric of his cases, and arguably the best-loved. Even the dog, in the end, turns out to have a rather more ordinary explanation than the legend deserved.
