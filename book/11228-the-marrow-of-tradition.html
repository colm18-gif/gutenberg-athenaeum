Returning Voices

The Marrow of Tradition

by Charles W. Chesnutt

A Southern town’s fragile racial truce collapses into a massacre based closely on an actual historical event Chesnutt had watched happen in his own lifetime. He wrote it while the wounds were still fresh, and it cost him readers who preferred their history gentler. It remains one of the most direct confrontations with that violence written by anyone who lived through its era.
