Amenities of Literature

by Isaac Disraeli

Sketches of the history of English literature, from the first manuscripts to the age of Elizabeth, published in 1841. Disraeli spent his life in libraries and his son Benjamin went into politics. Both, in their ways, were collectors of curiosities.
