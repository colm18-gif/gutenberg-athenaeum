Godey’s Lady’s Book, the most popular American magazine of the mid-nineteenth century, with hand-coloured fashion plates, recipes, patterns and fiction. Its editor, Sarah Josepha Hale, campaigned for years to make Thanksgiving a national holiday, and won. She also wrote “Mary Had a Little Lamb”.
