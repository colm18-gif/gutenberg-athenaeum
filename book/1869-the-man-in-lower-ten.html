The Man in Lower Ten

by Mary Roberts Rinehart

A young lawyer carrying important papers wakes in the wrong berth of a night sleeper and finds the man in his own berth, lower ten, murdered. Then the train is wrecked. Rinehart’s mystery is fast and funny, and a sleeping car is the ideal place to read it.
