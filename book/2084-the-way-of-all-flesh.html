The Way of All Flesh

by Samuel Butler

Four generations of the Pontifex family, and above all Ernest, crushed by a clergyman father and a pious mother and getting free of them very slowly. Samuel Butler, who also wrote Erewhon, worked on it for years and left it to be published after his death, in 1903. It takes the Victorian family apart from the inside.
