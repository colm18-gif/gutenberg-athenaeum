The Enchanted April

by Elizabeth von Arnim

Four Englishwomen who barely know one another rent a small medieval castle in Italy for April, largely because of the wisteria. Very little happens and everything changes; von Arnim’s comedy is gentle, but she knows exactly how unhappy her characters were at home.
