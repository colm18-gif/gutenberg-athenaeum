The Surrender of Napoleon

by Frederick Lewis Maitland

After Waterloo Napoleon tried to escape to America, but the Royal Navy was watching the coast, and on 15 July 1815 he came aboard HMS Bellerophon off Rochefort and gave himself up to her captain, Frederick Maitland. Maitland carried him to England, where he was told he would be sent to St Helena. This is Maitland’s own narrative of those three weeks, published in 1826.
