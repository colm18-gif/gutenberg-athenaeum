Through the Looking-Glass

by Lewis Carroll

A girl steps through a mirror into a world run entirely on the logic of chess, where you must run as fast as you can simply to remain in the same place. Carroll built the sequel around a stricter, stranger set of rules than the first book’s looser dream logic. The looking-glass insects alone are worth the visit.
