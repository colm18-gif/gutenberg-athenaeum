A Tour in Ireland

by Arthur Young

Arthur Young, the English writer on farming, toured Ireland between 1776 and 1779, noting rents, crops, wages and the state of the poor county by county. He was blunt about landlords and tenants alike. This is Henry Morley’s shortened edition.
