Tortoises

by D. H. Lawrence

Six poems about tortoises, published in New York in 1921 and later in Lawrence’s Birds, Beasts and Flowers: a baby tortoise setting out alone, the pattern on a shell, a tortoise family, and tortoises courting. Lawrence watched them in an Italian garden with great attention and some amusement. They are left for whoever waits for Jonathan.
