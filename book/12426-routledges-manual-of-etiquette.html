Routledge's Manual of Etiquette

by George Routledge

A small manual issued under the name of the London publisher George Routledge, on calls, balls, dinners and conversation for ladies and gentlemen. Its advice on giving a dinner is brisk. It is one of a flood of such books, written for readers who had come up in the world and wished to look as if they always had been.
