My Arctic Journal

by Josephine Peary

Josephine Diebitsch Peary went north with her husband Robert Peary in 1891 and spent the winter in a small house on McCormick Bay in north Greenland. This is her journal of that year, published in 1893. She was among the first white women to winter so far north, and she enjoyed most of it.
