Returning Voices

The Quest of the Silver Fleece

by W. E. B. Du Bois

A young woman with an education and a plan for cooperative Black land ownership meets fierce, organised resistance from those who profit by keeping that land divided. Du Bois used a novel where his essays could not quite reach the readers he wanted. It is optimistic in exactly the places his nonfiction rarely allowed itself to be.
