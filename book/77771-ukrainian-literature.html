Ukrainian Literature

by Clarence A. Manning

Clarence Manning’s studies of the leading Ukrainian writers, from Skovoroda and Kotliarevsky to Franko, Lesia Ukrainka, Kotsiubynsky and Stefanyk, published in 1944. Watson Kirkconnell’s foreword begins by calling the Ukrainians “the Irish of the Slavonic world”, which the Irish Room would not dispute. It is a good first map of the racks behind it.
