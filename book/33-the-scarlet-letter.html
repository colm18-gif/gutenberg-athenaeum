The Scarlet Letter

by Nathaniel Hawthorne

A woman wears a badge of shame sewn by the very people most implicated in her disgrace, and outlasts every one of their reputations by simple, stubborn dignity. The letter never quite means what the town insists it means. Hawthorne understood that public punishment mostly punishes the audience’s own conscience.
