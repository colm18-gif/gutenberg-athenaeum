The Analects of Confucius

by Confucius

The sayings of Confucius and his disciples, gathered by their followers after his death in 479 BC and for two thousand years at the heart of a Chinese education. Most entries are a few lines long: a question, an answer, now and then a rebuke. It is a book to open anywhere.
