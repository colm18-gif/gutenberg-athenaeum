The Stereoscope

by David Brewster

Brewster improved the stereoscope with lenses, and here he gives its history and theory: two pictures, one for each eye, made one picture with depth in the mind. Victorian parlours filled up with them. He also takes the chance to dispute Wheatstone’s claim to the invention.
