The Naturalist in Nicaragua

by Thomas Belt

Thomas Belt, a mining engineer, spent four years at the gold mines of Chontales and watched the forest round them with great care. He described the leaf-cutting ants that farm a fungus and the acacias that keep ants as bodyguards. Darwin admired the book greatly.
