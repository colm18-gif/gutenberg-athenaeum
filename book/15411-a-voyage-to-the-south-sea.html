A Voyage to the South Sea

by William Bligh

The fuller account of the whole voyage, published in 1792: the long passage out, the failure to round Cape Horn, the five months at Tahiti gathering breadfruit plants, the mutiny and the launch. The breadfruit was the point of the voyage: it was to feed the enslaved workers of the British West Indies. Bligh brought it there on his second voyage, in 1793.
