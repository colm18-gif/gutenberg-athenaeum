Table Traits, with Something on Them

by John Doran

John Doran, an editor and historian of the theatre, gathered this book of anecdotes about eating and drinking in 1854: feasts, cooks, gluttons and table customs from antiquity to his own day. It is gossip with footnotes. Like most Victorian table-talk, it believes nearly everything it was told.
