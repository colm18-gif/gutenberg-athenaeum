The Mystery of the Yellow Room

by Gaston Leroux

A scream in the night, a shot, and a young woman found injured in a room locked from the inside, with her attacker nowhere to be found. Before The Phantom of the Opera, Leroux wrote this classic locked-room puzzle, solved by an eighteen-year-old reporter. The floor plans are part of the fun.
