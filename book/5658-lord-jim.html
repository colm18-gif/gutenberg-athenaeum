Lord Jim

by Joseph Conrad

A young officer abandons a pilgrim ship he believes is sinking, and spends the rest of his life trying to recover from that one moment. Conrad’s Marlow tells the story in pieces, on verandas after dinner. It is a book about honour, and whether a single failure can ever be undone.
