An Historical Journal of the Transactions at Port Jackson and Norfolk Island

by John Hunter

John Hunter was captain of HMS Sirius in the First Fleet, and this is his account of the first years of the colony, published in 1793 with Philip Gidley King’s journal of founding the settlement on Norfolk Island in March 1788. The Sirius was wrecked on the reef at Norfolk Island on 19 March 1790, and Hunter and his crew were stranded there for most of a year. It is the island’s own beginning, told by the men who were there.
