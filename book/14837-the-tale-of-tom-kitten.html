The Tale of Tom Kitten

by Beatrix Potter

Mrs. Tabitha Twitchit dresses her three kittens in their best clothes for a tea party and tells them to keep clean. They do not. A five-minute story with easy sentences and some wonderful words, like “pinafore” and “tippet”.
