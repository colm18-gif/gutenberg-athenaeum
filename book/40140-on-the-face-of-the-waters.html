On the Face of the Waters

by Flora Annie Steel

Flora Annie Steel’s novel of the Indian Rebellion of 1857, published in 1896. She based it on the accounts of survivors and those who had been there. The Mutiny, as it was called then, was the largest rising against the East India Company.
