The Crisis, Vol. 1, No. 1, November, 1910

by Various

The first number of The Crisis, the magazine of the NAACP, founded and edited by W. E. B. Du Bois in 1910. It reported lynching, discrimination and achievement with equal force, and later published the young writers of the Harlem Renaissance. A thousand copies of this first number were printed.
