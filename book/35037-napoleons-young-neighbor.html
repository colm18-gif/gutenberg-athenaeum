Napoleon’s Young Neighbor

by Helen Leah Reed

When Napoleon landed on St Helena in October 1815, Longwood was not ready for him, and he spent his first weeks in a pavilion in the garden of The Briars, the house of William Balcombe, of the East India Company. The Balcombes’ daughter Betsy, a girl of about thirteen who spoke French, teased him, beat him at games and became his friend. This book tells her story for young readers.
