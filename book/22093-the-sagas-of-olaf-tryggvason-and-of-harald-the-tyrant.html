The Sagas of Olaf Tryggvason and of Harald the Tyrant

by Snorri Sturluson

Two of the sagas of the kings of Norway from Snorri Sturluson’s Heimskringla, in an illustrated edition. Olaf Tryggvason was the king who sent Sigmund Brestisson home to the Faroes to bring the islands to Christianity, about the year 1000. Snorri is the best storyteller of the Norse historians, and these two kings gave him a great deal to tell.
