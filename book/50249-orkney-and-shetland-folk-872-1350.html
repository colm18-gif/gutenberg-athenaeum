Orkney and Shetland Folk 872-1350

by Alfred Wintle Johnston

A short history of the Norse earldom of Orkney and Shetland, from its founding to the fourteenth century, by the founder of the Viking Society for Northern Research. The Faroes were its neighbour to the north, settled by the same people. Johnston writes of land, law and family with the care of an antiquary.
