The Ghost Pirates

by William Hope Hodgson

A seaman who joined a ship nobody else would sign on tells of shadows that climb out of the sea and up the rigging. Hodgson had been a sailor himself and disliked it thoroughly. It was published in 1909, nine years before he was killed at Ypres.
