Beyond Good and Evil

by Friedrich Nietzsche

A philosopher takes a hammer, figuratively, to every value he considers inherited rather than earned, and dares the reader to see what is actually left standing afterward. Nietzsche wrote in aphorisms rather than arguments, which makes him easy to quote and considerably harder to summarise fairly. Read him in full or not at all; the fragments alone have caused enough mischief.
