Tales of the Punjab

by Flora Annie Steel

Flora Annie Steel’s collection of Punjabi folk tales, published in 1894 with illustrations by John Lockwood Kipling, Rudyard Kipling’s father. She heard them in the villages where her husband served. They are stories for the evenings, with a talking jackal in most of them.
