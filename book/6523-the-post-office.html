The Post Office

by Rabindranath Tagore

A play about Amal, a sick boy kept indoors, who watches the world go by the window and dreams of the letter the king will send him. It is in Tagore’s English from the Bengali Dak Ghar and was staged at the Abbey Theatre in Dublin in 1913. It is short, and it is not as simple as it looks.
