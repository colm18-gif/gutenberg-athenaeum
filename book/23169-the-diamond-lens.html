The Diamond Lens

by Fitz James O’Brien

O’Brien’s story of 1858 is about a microscopist who makes a lens of perfect power and sees, in a drop of water, a whole world, and a woman in it he can never reach. It is one of the first great American stories of science fiction. A tiny figure seen through glass: the right story to find on this table.
