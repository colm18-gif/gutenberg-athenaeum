Andersen’s Fairy Tales

by Hans Christian Andersen

Original fairy tales, invented rather than collected, many of them considerably sadder than the folk tradition they were written alongside. Andersen understood that the best children’s stories do not flinch from grief, only from cruelty for its own sake. The mermaid does not get the ending the songs about her usually promise.
