Bartleby, the Scrivener

by Herman Melville

A Wall Street lawyer hires a pale, neat, quiet copyist who, asked one day to check a document, replies: “I would prefer not to.” After that he prefers not to do anything else. Melville’s story is funny, then strange, then very sad.
