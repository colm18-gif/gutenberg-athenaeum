Insectivorous Plants

by Charles Darwin

Darwin fed the sundew on scraps of meat, egg, hair and glass to find out what it would digest and what it would merely hold. He found that its tentacles could feel a fragment of hair weighing a tiny fraction of a milligram. In 1860 he told Lyell that he cared more about Drosera than about the origin of all the species in the world.
