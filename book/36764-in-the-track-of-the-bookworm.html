In the Track of the Bookworm

by Irving Browne

A rambling, affectionate account of a lifelong reader’s wanderings through secondhand bookshops and the odd, unrepeatable finds waiting inside them. Browne wrote for other people who understood exactly why a battered old volume could be worth an entire afternoon’s detour. I recognise the type of reader he is describing rather too well.
