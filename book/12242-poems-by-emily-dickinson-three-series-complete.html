Poems by Emily Dickinson, Three Series, Complete

by Emily Dickinson

All three of the first series in one volume. About ten of her poems were printed in her lifetime, all without her name; after her death in May 1886 her sister Lavinia found the rest in her room, nearly eighteen hundred of them, many sewn by hand into little booklets. Lavinia had been asked to burn the letters, and did, but nothing had been said about poems.
