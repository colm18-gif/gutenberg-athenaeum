The Italian Cook Book

by Maria Gentile

Published in New York in 1919 for American households, when Italian cooking was spreading through the cities where Italian families had settled. It gives soups, pastas, risottos, sauces, pastries and sweets with American measures. Its preface hopes to persuade readers that Italian cooking is both good and thrifty.
