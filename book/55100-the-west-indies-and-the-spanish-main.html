The West Indies and the Spanish Main

by Anthony Trollope

The Post Office sent Trollope to the West Indies in 1858 to put their mails in order, and he wrote this book on the way, from Jamaica and Cuba to Barbados, Trinidad, Demerara and Panama. He thought it the best book that had come from his pen, and it is often very funny. It is also full of the opinions on race of a man of his time, stated without embarrassment; Froudacity, across the room, is the answer to books like it.
