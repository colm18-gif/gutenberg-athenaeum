A Sentimental Journey Through France and Italy

by Laurence Sterne

Parson Yorick sets out across France meaning to describe it, and describes his own feelings instead, mostly about the women he meets. Sterne published it in 1768, weeks before he died, and it never reaches Italy. It is short, tender and full of dashes.
