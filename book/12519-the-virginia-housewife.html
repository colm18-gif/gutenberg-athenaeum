The Virginia Housewife

by Mary Randolph

Mary Randolph kept a well-known boarding house in Richmond, and her book of 1824 is often called the first regional American cookbook. Gumbo, okra soup and catfish are among its recipes, drawn from English, French, Spanish and African cooking. In houses like hers the cooking was largely done by enslaved people, whose names the book does not give.
