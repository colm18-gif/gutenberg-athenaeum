The Bird Watcher in the Shetlands

by Edmund Selous

Edmund Selous spent the summers of the early 1900s watching the sea-birds and seals of Shetland, and wrote down what they did, hour by hour, rather than shooting them, which was not yet usual. The birds of the Faroes are the same: puffins, guillemots, gannets and skuas. His book is patient, sharp and often funny.
