The Story of the Treasure Seekers

by E. Nesbit

The six Bastable children decide to restore their family’s fortune by digging for treasure, becoming detectives, and selling their poetry to a newspaper. One of them tells the story and tries very hard to hide which one. It is very funny, and the English is lively without being hard.
