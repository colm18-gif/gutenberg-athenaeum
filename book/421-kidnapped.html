Kidnapped

by Robert Louis Stevenson

A young heir is kidnapped by his own uncle to be sold into servitude, and escapes into a genuinely dangerous alliance with a man on the wrong side of a rebellion. Stevenson wrote friendship here as something that survives despite serious, permanent political disagreement. That is rarer in fiction than the shipwreck that starts it.
