The Works of Edgar Allan Poe — Volume 3

by Edgar Allan Poe

The third volume of the Raven Edition holds the whole of Poe’s only novel, The Narrative of Arthur Gordon Pym, followed by Ligeia, Morella and a few comic tales. Pym begins as a sea story and ends somewhere near the South Pole, in a mist that has puzzled readers ever since. Poe died in Baltimore at forty, in circumstances nobody has ever quite explained.
