Songs of Vidyapati

by Vidyapati

Songs of the love of Radha and Krishna by Vidyapati, a poet of Mithila in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries who wrote in Maithili. This is the English translation of 1915 by Ananda Coomaraswamy and Arun Sen. They were sung in Bengal for centuries after.
