Poems by Emily Dickinson, Series One

by Emily Dickinson

The first book of her poems, published in Boston in 1890, four years after she died, and edited by Mabel Loomis Todd and Thomas Wentworth Higginson. The editors gave the poems titles and smoothed some of her rhymes and punctuation, which later editions put back. It was reprinted again and again within months, to the surprise of nearly everyone concerned.
