The Mysterious Affair at Styles

by Agatha Christie

A retired soldier moves in with an old friend just as that friend’s wealthy stepmother is poisoned in a locked room. It was Christie’s very first novel, and the detective introduced in it would go on to solve rather a lot more of these for her. Watch how confidently the trick is already assembled, even this early.
