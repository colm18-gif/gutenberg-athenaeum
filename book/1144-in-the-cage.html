In the Cage

by Henry James

A telegraph girl overhears the whole of upper-class London’s love affairs, one coded message at a time, while being permitted none of her own. She knows everyone’s secrets and owns none of the life attached to them. James spends the whole book on the difference between watching a story and living inside one.
