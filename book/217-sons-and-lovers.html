Sons and Lovers

by D. H. Lawrence

Paul Morel grows up in a Nottinghamshire mining village between a father who drinks and a mother who puts all her hopes in her sons. Lawrence’s novel of 1913 is close to his own life, and his mother died while he was writing it. Neither of the young women Paul loves stands a chance against her.
