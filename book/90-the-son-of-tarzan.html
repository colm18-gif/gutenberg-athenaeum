The Son of Tarzan

by Edgar Rice Burroughs

The son of the jungle-raised lord grows up in London civilisation and discovers, with some relief, that the jungle was rather more honest. Burroughs kept mining that same vein of ancestry and instinct book after book, and readers kept buying it. Do not read it for subtlety; read it for momentum.
