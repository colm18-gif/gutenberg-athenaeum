New Arabian Nights

by Robert Louis Stevenson

Stevenson’s first book of stories, published in 1882, sends Prince Florizel of Bohemia through London at night in disguise, as Harun al-Rashid goes through Baghdad in the Nights. In The Suicide Club he joins a club whose members draw cards each night to see which of them will die, and which of them will do the killing. The Rajah’s Diamond follows a jewel from hand to hand.
