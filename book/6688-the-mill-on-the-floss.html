The Mill on the Floss

by George Eliot

Clever, passionate Maggie Tulliver grows up beside the river Floss with her brother Tom, whom she loves more than anyone. Eliot remembers childhood as well as any writer ever has. The sentences are long but worth it, and the ending is unforgettable.
