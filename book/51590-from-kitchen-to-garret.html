From Kitchen to Garret

by J. E. Panton

Jane Ellen Panton, daughter of the painter W. P. Frith, wrote this in 1888 for young couples setting up house on a modest income, with one or two servants: how to furnish, decorate and run a home, room by room, from the kitchen up. It sold very well. Its opinions on wallpaper are firm.
