The Tale of Benjamin Bunny

by Beatrix Potter

Peter Rabbit’s cousin Benjamin takes him back into Mr. McGregor’s garden to fetch the clothes Peter lost there. It goes about as well as you would expect. A very short story in clear, simple English, with a good fright in the middle.
