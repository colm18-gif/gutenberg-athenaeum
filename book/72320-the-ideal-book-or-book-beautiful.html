The Ideal Book or Book Beautiful

by T. J. Cobden-Sanderson

Cobden-Sanderson founded the Doves Bindery and later the Doves Press, and this short essay sets out what he thought a book should be: type, page, binding and all, working together. He meant it as a manifesto. In the end he threw the Doves type into the Thames rather than let anyone else use it.
