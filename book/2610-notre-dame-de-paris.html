Notre-Dame de Paris

by Victor Hugo

In medieval Paris, the bell-ringer Quasimodo, the dancer Esmeralda and the archdeacon Frollo circle around the great cathedral, which is the real hero of the book. Hugo wrote it partly to stop Gothic buildings being pulled down, and it helped. English readers know it as The Hunchback of Notre-Dame.
