Egyptian Tales, Translated from the Papyri

by W. M. Flinders Petrie

Stories from Egyptian papyri of the Old and Middle Kingdoms, translated by the archaeologist Flinders Petrie in 1895. Among them are the tale of Sinuhe, an official who flees Egypt and longs all his life to be buried there, and stories of magicians told at the court of King Khufu. They are among the oldest stories in the world.
