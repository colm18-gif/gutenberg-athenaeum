The Wind in the Rose-Bush

by Mary E. Wilkins Freeman

Six New England ghost stories of 1903, among them Luella Miller, in which the people who look after a languid young widow sicken one by one. Freeman’s hauntings keep to kitchens and spare rooms, among women who would rather not make a fuss. She was better known for her quiet stories of village life, A New England Nun among them.
