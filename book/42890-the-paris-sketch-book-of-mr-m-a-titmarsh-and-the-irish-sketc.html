The Paris Sketch Book of Mr. M. A. Titmarsh; and the Irish Sketch Book

by William Makepeace Thackeray

Thackeray toured Ireland in 1842 and wrote The Irish Sketch Book under the name Michael Angelo Titmarsh; it is bound here with his Paris Sketch Book. He is a comic, curious and sometimes careless traveller. It is a picture of the country three years before the Famine.
