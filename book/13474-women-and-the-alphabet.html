Women and the Alphabet

by Thomas Wentworth Higginson

Essays on the rights of women, the first of them printed in the Atlantic Monthly in 1859 as Ought Women to Learn the Alphabet? Higginson argued for women’s education and the vote for half a century. It was an article of his in the same magazine, a Letter to a Young Contributor, that made Emily Dickinson write to him.
