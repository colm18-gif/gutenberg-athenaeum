Tales of Men and Ghosts

by Edith Wharton

Ten stories of 1910, among them Afterward, in which an American couple rent a house in Dorset hoping it has a ghost, and are told they will not know it until long afterwards. Wharton set her ghosts among comfortable people. They are the more unsettling for the good furniture.
