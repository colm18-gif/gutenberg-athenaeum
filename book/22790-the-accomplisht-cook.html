The Accomplisht Cook

by Robert May

Robert May was sent to France to train as a boy and spent his working life cooking in great English houses, and he published this in 1660, the year Charles II came home. It is a book of feasts on a grand scale. For Twelfth Night he describes pies that hide live frogs and birds, to jump and fly out when the lids are lifted.
