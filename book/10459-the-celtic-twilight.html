The Celtic Twilight

by W. B. Yeats

Yeats’s little book of 1893: stories of fairies, ghosts and visions, heard from country people in Sligo and Galway. It gave its name to a whole mood of Irish writing. The pieces are short, some only a page, and are best read by the fire.
