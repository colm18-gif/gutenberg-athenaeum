The Piazza Tales

by Herman Melville

Six stories by Melville, among them Bartleby, Benito Cereno and The Encantadas, ten sketches of the Galápagos. Melville had been among the islands as a young sailor on the whaler Acushnet, in 1841. The sketches are of cinders, tortoises and the people who were left there.
