The Confessions of St. Augustine

by Saint Augustine

A bishop spends several hundred pages arguing with his own younger self, and loses more often than you would expect from a saint. The famous line about restless hearts is worth the whole detour through his adolescence. It is the oldest kind of memoir this library keeps: a man watching himself become someone else.
