The Flying U Ranch

by B. M. Bower

A rough-and-ready ranch hand and cattle outfit navigate cattle drives, rivalries, and range politics across the American West. Bower wrote frontier fiction with genuine affection for the unglamorous daily labour underneath the gunfights. It is a working ranch first and an adventure story second, which is rather to its credit.
