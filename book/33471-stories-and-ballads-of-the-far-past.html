Stories and Ballads of the Far Past

by Nora K. Chadwick

Nora Kershaw (later Nora Chadwick) translated these sagas from the Norse in 1921, and beside them Faroese ballads on the same stories, among them the ballads of Nornagest and of Arngrim’s sons. So far as she knew, no Faroese ballad had been put into English before. The kvæði, as the Faroese call them, are still sung in the islands to the chain dance.
