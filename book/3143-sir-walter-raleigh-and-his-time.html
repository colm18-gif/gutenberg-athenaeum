Sir Walter Raleigh and His Time

by Charles Kingsley

Walter Raleigh was given Lismore and great tracts of Waterford and Cork in the plantation of Munster, and sold them in 1602 to Richard Boyle, Robert Boyle’s father. Charles Kingsley’s long essay is a Victorian admirer’s portrait of the man and his age. The library’s copy of Raleigh’s own Discovery of Guiana stands beside it.
