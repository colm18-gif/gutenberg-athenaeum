The Naturalist on the River Amazons

by Henry Walter Bates

Henry Walter Bates spent eleven years on the Amazon, from 1848 to 1859, and sent home more than fourteen thousand species, eight thousand of them new to science. He had set out with Alfred Russel Wallace, and Darwin called this the best book of natural history travels ever published in England. It is just as good on the people he lived among and the river towns.
