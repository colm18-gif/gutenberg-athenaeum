Candide

by Voltaire

A relentlessly optimistic young man is dragged through every disaster the eighteenth century could arrange, purely to mock a philosophy that insists it is all for the best. It is very funny and, underneath that, quite furious. Voltaire wrote it fast, and it reads that way, in the best sense.
