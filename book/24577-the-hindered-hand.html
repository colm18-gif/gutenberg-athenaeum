The Hindered Hand

by Sutton E. Griggs

A Black family in the South tries to live within a system designed to break it, while the novel argues openly with the white supremacist fiction then selling in its hundreds of thousands. Griggs published his own novels and sold many of them himself. This one appeared in 1905.
