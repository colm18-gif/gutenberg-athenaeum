Jacob’s Room

by Virginia Woolf

A life told from its edges: Jacob Flanders as a boy at the seaside, at Cambridge, in London and in Greece, always seen by someone else. Virginia Woolf’s third novel, of 1922, was her first clean break with the ordinary novel. The empty room at the end says what the book will not.
