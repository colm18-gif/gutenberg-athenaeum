The Battle of Life

by Charles Dickens

The fourth Christmas book, of 1846, written in Switzerland while Dickens was also at work on Dombey and Son. Two sisters live with their father, Doctor Jeddler, on an old battlefield, and he holds that the whole of life is a farce; then the younger vanishes on the night her betrothed comes home. It is the only one of the five without anything supernatural, and the one readers have forgotten.
