Twenty-Two Goblins

by Arthur W. Ryder

Arthur Ryder’s English translation of the Sanskrit Vetalapanchavimshati, a cycle of tales told to a king by a goblin who hangs from a tree. At the end of each story the goblin asks the king a riddle that he must answer. The framework is old, and the tales have been retold in many languages since.
