The First Voyage Round the World

by Antonio Pigafetta

Antonio Pigafetta sailed with Magellan in 1519 and was one of eighteen men who came home on the Victoria three years later. His journal records the strait, the crossing of the Pacific and Magellan’s death in the Philippines. He also noted words from the languages he heard on the way.
