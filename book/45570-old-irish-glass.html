Old Irish Glass

by Graydon Stannus

George and William Penrose opened their glassworks on the quay at Waterford in 1783, and Waterford glass became famous for its cutting. Mrs Graydon Stannus’s book is a collector’s guide to the old Irish glasshouses. The Penroses’ works closed in 1851; the name was made again in Waterford from 1947.
