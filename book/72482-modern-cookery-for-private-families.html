Modern Cookery for Private Families

by Eliza Acton

Eliza Acton was a poet before she was a cook, and her book, first published in 1845 and revised over the years after, set a pattern later books followed: a list of the ingredients and the time it takes after every recipe. Isabella Beeton borrowed from it freely. Her prose is plain, exact and occasionally sharp.
