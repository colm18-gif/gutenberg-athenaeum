Harlem Shadows

by Claude McKay

Claude McKay was born in Jamaica in 1889 and had published two books of verse in Jamaican Creole before he left for the United States in 1912. Harlem Shadows, of 1922, is the book that made his name in New York. Among its sonnets of anger, If We Must Die among them, are poems of longing for the island he left, The Tropics in New York the best known.
