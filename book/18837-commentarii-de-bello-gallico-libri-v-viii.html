Commentarii de Bello Gallico, Libri V-VIII

by Julius Caesar

The second half of the Gallic War: a second landing in Britain, revolts in the north and the great rising under Vercingetorix, which ends at the siege of Alesia. The eighth book is not Caesar’s own; his officer Aulus Hirtius finished the story after him. It reads as plainly as a report, and is meant to.
