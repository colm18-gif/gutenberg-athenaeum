The Haunted Man and the Ghost’s Bargain

by Charles Dickens

The last of the Christmas books, of 1848: Redlaw, a learned chemist, sits by his fire on a winter night brooding on the wrongs and sorrows of his past. A phantom in his own likeness offers to take his memory of them away, and to let him pass the gift on to everyone he meets. He takes it, and finds that he has lost far more than he meant to.
