The Lost Continent

by C. J. Cutcliffe Hyne

A group of shipwrecked sailors discover a lost civilisation practicing an especially inventive brand of human sacrifice. It is unapologetic Victorian adventure fiction, more concerned with incident than insight. Do not look here for subtlety; look here for a very long night’s entertainment.
