Natural History

Wake-Robin

by John Burroughs

John Burroughs’s first book of nature essays, mostly about birds, written while he was a clerk at the Treasury in Washington. His friend Walt Whitman suggested the title. Burroughs became the best-loved nature writer in America, with a cabin in the woods above the Hudson and visitors who included Theodore Roosevelt.
