The Communist Manifesto

by Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels

A short, furious pamphlet arguing that history is the story of who owns what, and that the current arrangement is neither natural nor permanent. It was meant to be read aloud at meetings, not studied quietly at a desk, and you can feel that urgency in every sentence. Few documents this brief have rearranged this much of the world since.
