The International Jewish Cook Book

by Florence Kreisler Greenbaum

Sixteen hundred recipes that keep the Jewish dietary laws, with the rules for kashering, for American households whose families had come from many countries. Florence Kreisler Greenbaum gathered dishes for the Sabbath and the festivals alongside everyday cooking. It is a cookbook of migration as well as of law.
