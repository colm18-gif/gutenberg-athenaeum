Folk-Tales of Bengal

by Lal Behari Day

Tales collected in the villages of Bengal by the Reverend Lal Behari Day and published in 1883. Day was a Bengali Christian clergyman who gathered them from the people about him. They are full of ghosts, demons, sharp wives and foolish kings.
