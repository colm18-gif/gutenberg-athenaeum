Deccan Nursery Tales

by Charles Augustus Kincaid

Fairy tales from the Deccan plateau, collected and retold by Charles Kincaid in 1914. Kincaid was a judge in the Indian Civil Service and a student of Marathi history and folklore. They are the stories that Maharashtrian children heard at bedtime.
