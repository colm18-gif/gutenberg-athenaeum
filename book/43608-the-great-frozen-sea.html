The Great Frozen Sea

by Albert Hastings Markham

Albert Hastings Markham sailed with the British Arctic Expedition of 1875 and 1876 in the Alert, and in May 1876 led the sledge party that reached a farthest north of 83° 20′, dragging their boats over the pack. Most of his men were ill with scurvy before they got back. This is his account of the voyage.
