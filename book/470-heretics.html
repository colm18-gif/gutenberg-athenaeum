Philosophy

Heretics

by G. K. Chesterton

A collection of essays taking aim at the fashionable pessimists and cynics of his day, arguing cheerfully that their sophistication was mostly just despair with better manners. Chesterton preferred paradox to solemnity as a weapon, and wielded it constantly. He is more fun to disagree with than most writers are to agree with.
