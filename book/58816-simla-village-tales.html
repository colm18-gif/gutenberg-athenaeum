Simla Village Tales

by Alice Elizabeth Dracott

Folk tales collected by Alice Dracott in the hill villages round Simla and published in 1906. Simla was the summer capital of British India, and the villagers told her their stories while the officials above them held their dances. She gives them in plain English.
