Bookbinding, and the Care of Books

by Douglas Cockerell

A handbook of 1901 for amateurs, bookbinders and librarians, explaining how books are sewn, covered and kept from harm. Cockerell had learned his trade under T. J. Cobden-Sanderson. He thought a binding should first of all let the book open.
