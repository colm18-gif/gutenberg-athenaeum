Station Life in New Zealand

by Lady Barker

Letters home from Mary Anne Barker, who went out to Canterbury in 1865 with her second husband and lived on a sheep station in the hills above the plains. There are floods, a shearing, a ball, and the great snowstorm of 1867 that buried the sheep. It is cheerful, observant and still a pleasure to read.
