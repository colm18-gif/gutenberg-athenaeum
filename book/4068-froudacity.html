Froudacity

by J. J. Thomas

In 1888 the historian James Anthony Froude published a book on his tour of the British West Indies, arguing that their Black people were unfit to govern themselves. John Jacob Thomas, a schoolmaster from Trinidad who had written the first grammar of its Creole, answered him point by point in this book of 1889. Froude’s book stands on the shelf across the room, so that the reply can be read beside it.
