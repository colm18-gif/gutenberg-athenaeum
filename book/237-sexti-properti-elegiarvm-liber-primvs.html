Sexti Properti Elegiarvm: Liber Primvs

by Propertius

The first book of elegies by Propertius, almost all about Cynthia, who is by turns cruel, faithless, adored and asleep. It begins with her name: “Cynthia prima suis miserum me cepit ocellis.” Ezra Pound loved him, and wrote a famous and much-argued-over homage.
