Ivanhoe: A Romance

by Walter Scott

A disinherited knight returns from crusade in disguise to find his country’s Norman and Saxon halves still refusing to speak to each other. Scott more or less invented the modern costume drama with this one. The tournament scenes alone are why half the era’s illustrators kept their jobs.
