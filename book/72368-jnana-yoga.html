Jnana Yoga

by Swami Vivekananda

Lectures by Swami Vivekananda given in the West in the 1890s, on the Vedanta philosophy of knowledge. Vivekananda was a disciple of Ramakrishna and was the first Indian teacher to speak to a large Western audience. He spoke in English, and the lectures are printed as they were taken down.
