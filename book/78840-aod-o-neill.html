Aoḋ Ó Néill

by Pádraig Ó Seaghdha

A book in Irish about Aodh Ó Néill, Hugh O’Neill, the Earl of Tyrone who led the Nine Years’ War against Elizabeth I. Its author, Pádraig Ó Seaghdha, wrote as Conán Maol and was one of the busiest writers of the early Gaelic League. It is printed in the Gaelic type, with dotted letters.
