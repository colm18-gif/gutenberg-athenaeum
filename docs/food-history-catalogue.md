# The Gastronomic Athenaeum: Gutenberg numbers checked

Every title named in the food-history project brief, looked up in Project Gutenberg's own catalogue
(`pg_catalog.csv`) by the Catalogue lookup workflow on 13 November 2026. A number here is the catalogue's, not a
guess; whether a book is on a shelf is decided in `data/new-books.js` (rooms `food-*`), where the New books workflow
also checks each text's own title and author.

Four numbers first used in `food-history-wing.js` named other books: 6501 is Diderot's *Supplément au Voyage de
Bougainville*, 12350 is *The International Jewish Cook Book* (not *Every Step in Canning*), 44915 is *Margaret
Brown's French Cookery Book* (not *The Physiology of Taste*) and 16035 is Brandon Head's *The Food of the Gods*, on
cocoa (not Hazlitt).
